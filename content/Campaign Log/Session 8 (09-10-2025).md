*Luminar 102 (Morning), Morenthia*  
[[@Jack]], [[@Ichoren]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Cove-Candle]]

Heading to the [[Merchant's Assembly]], the party soon arrived at the heart of the city. Here the streets opened up into a large, cobblestone square—an area known as **The Seat**, the location of several of the city's key buildings. 
 
Ordinarily, The Seat would be alive with color and noise: market stalls bursting with silks and spices, hawkers shouting the price of wares, children darting between carts, and musicians filling the air with song. But now the party found the square was eerily bare, and they saw the wooden frames of abandoned stalls and the small remnants of their contents rotted and unusable.

At the square's centre was a grand fountain, but the sound of its flowing water was drowned out by the rising din of a nearby crowd.

Hundreds of refugees gathered in the shadow of [[City Hall]], their faces stern, their clothes travel-stained, their voices raised in anger and despair. The party saw several women clutched hungry children and called for bread, old men shook their fists toward the hall's great oak doors, and several young men shouted curses at the city’s rulers within. The air was thick with sweat and desperation, punctuated by wafts of acrid smoke from small campfires that smoldered where the refugees had lingered for days.

At the head of the crowd, and direction of their anger, was a set of wide, heavy stone steps that led up to the large doors of [[City Hall]]. Several [[Shining Daggers]] stood upon the steps in loose formation, and held their spears stiff to their chests and watched the crowd with uneasy eyes, prepared to hold back the throng that pressed ever closer.

On the other side of the square loomed the ostentatious [[Merchant's Assembly]], its huge polished brass doors firmly closed and ornate windows shuttered from within.

The party pushed through the edges of the crowd and made their way towards the [[Merchant's Assembly]]. On their way, [[@Jack]] encountered a young male who was riled up and angry, and complained loudly about how the mayor was not looking after the refugees and that they starved whilst the rich enjoyed luxuries in their grand buildings. After a few words of encouragement in his ear from [[@Jack]], the young refugee planted himself on an upturned crate and began to chant, "Rush the hall! Rush the hall!" The chant caught and rippled like the waves of a flame through the crowd; a spark that ignited and quickly took hold. Discontent turned to anger. Anger turned to violence. Missiles were thrown, and the pot boiled over.

As the party tried to escape the situation and get themselves to the [[Merchant's Assembly]], bad turned to worse. They saw a brick arched up into the air and crashed through one of [[City Hall]]'s great windows.

In response, one of the [[Shining Daggers]] appeared from behind the line, but held not a spear but a heavy crossbow. He slowly brought this to aim at the instigator in the crowd—the recipient of [[@Jack]]'s words of encouragement. The sound of the loosed bolt was not heard over the crowd, but the impact was seen. The poor fellow looked down at his chest, a pained expression on his face, before he fell forward lifeless on the hard stone floor. The crowd silenced, stunned. Then screams erupted and pandemonium set in. People panicked and fled, and the [[Shining Daggers]] stepped forward and reigned down brutal blows using the shafts of their spears on any soul unfortunately enough to be in their path.

Having realised the carnage that now unfolded before them, the party urgently tried to find a way into the [[Merchant's Assembly]]. [[@Zharek]], scarred from the previous day's unfortunate encounter with [[@Jack]]'s blade, flew up to the first floor to get a look inside. He signed to the party that he saw a lone male cowering inside near the doors, tending the otherwise unoccupied building. [[Tibbin (Tibs) Figleaf|Tibs]] called in, and after hearing the voice of his dear friend, [[Finias Findlewart]] ushered them inside and barred the doors behind them. 

As screams echoed from the square outside, and the occasional bloodied body slammed against the windows, in the relative safety of the assembly hall the party explained how they'd rescued [[Tibbin (Tibs) Figleaf|Tibs]]. Finias was extremely grateful for them doing so, and happily agreed to take [[Tibbin (Tibs) Figleaf|Tibs]] in and keep him safe. [[Tibbin (Tibs) Figleaf|Tibs]] and [[@Ichoren]] parted with sadness, but in the knowledge that it was for the best.

Taking a back door from the [[Merchant's Assembly]], the party promptly made their way over to the docks to seek the boat promised to [[@Cove-Candle]].

The docks ordinarily bustled with merchant ships, riverboats, and the odd [[Riverguard]] patrol, but were now chaotic and fragmented. A few groups of sailors and merchants quarreled over the limited supplies. As they walked through, the party overheard a nearby sea captain who complained loudly that the Dockmaster's had seized his vessel, and told him it was needed for the emergency transport of goods. A noble expresses discontent that he was taxed an extortionate amount to dock, and forced to pay under threat of a blade. Several refugees slept rough in doorways and called out to the party for coin or safe passage. [[@Jack]] gave one family a ration (albeit this was really just to lighten his own load).

When the party arrived at the **Shipwright's Quay**, they found large enclosed shipyard that bore the sign of [[Oona Gentlebreeze]]. As they looked down the water's edge, they saw several ships were docked. They saw several [[Shining Daggers]] forced off a ship's crew at knifepoint, and threw one overboard. The mercenaries appeared to be led by a large one-eared orc who was garbed in a long black cloak that was pinned with the symbol of the [[Shining Daggers]]—but this one was golden. To the other side of the shipyard they saw a large tavern, and moored next to it was a dark and imposing ship that bore black coloured sails. Beyond this, raised on a small hill, was the [[Riverguard]] building.

Upon knocking on the shipyard's door, the party were greeted by [[Oona Gentlebreeze]], a middle-aged human female who was clothed in long dungarees and wore her hair tied up in a loose ponytail. She invited the party inside, away from prying eyes, and considered the note given to [[@Cove-Candle]] by [[Lord Ronald Squinketon|Lord Squinketon]]. She was happy to oblige and deliver a ship as promised, but explained that all her ships had been seized. She gave the party the following information.
- [[Denarin Varn]] had invoked so-called emergency powers to seize many ships, including hers. He was using the [[Shining Daggers]] to enforce this. They were led by [[Draz Steelfist]] (the orc the party described seeing nearby). She wasn't fond of [[Denarin Varn]] at all, and like most others couldn't understand how he had come to be in power.
- She was concerned that even if the party took a boat from her they would not make it past the [[Shining Daggers]], and if they did, they'd then have to contend with the [[Black Oars]] on the river.
- The [[Black Oars]] were outwardly a supposed band of merchants, but everyone knew they were really pirates who were led by **Blackbeak**.
- She had six ships available and—if the party could find a way—she would allow them to take one of these. She would need a few hours to make any repairs to their chosen vessel. Alternatively, she offered to discreetly make a new ship, but said it would take about a week. Reluctantly, she suggested they could try **Timmy Bentnail** next door who might make one quicker, but he wasn't exactly known for his fine craftmanship. 
- She showed the party the six vessels—a Fishing Boat, Barge, Sloop, Cutter, Schooner, and Brigantine—and talked the party through their various pros and cons and crew requirements. She told them to ponder their decision.

As the party discussed their options, the door to the shipyard burst open. Inside walked several mallards, clothed with flat caps upon their heads and woolen suits and waistcoats. A slim mallard swaggered over from the centre of the group. "Alroight lads," he said. "This shipyard is under new management, by order of the Beaky Blinders."

The mallard with the swagger was [[Tommy Billby]], and alongside him were his brothers Arthur and John. Despite their dramatic entrance, he explained the Blinders had no intention of taking over Oona's shipyard, but they did have a proposal for the party. He suggested a mutually beneficial arrangement—the party would find some evidence for the Blinders, and in exchange the Blinders would help them escape with their boat. The party found out the following information:
- The [[Beaky Blinders]] don't like [[Denarin Varn]]. They couldn't understand how he'd taken power and why even the nobles feared him, nor how he afforded the expensive services of the [[Shining Daggers]].
- They deduced that [[Denarin Varn]] must be working for someone else, and they wanted to know who the "puppet master" was. They believed there must be evidence of some communication between the parties at [[City Hall]], given [[Denarin Varn]] hadn't left since taking power. 
- They asked the party to get into [[City Hall]]—by whatever means they chose—and find evidence. They were to bring it back to the Blinders at the [[Wobbly Lantern]]. In exchange, the Blinders would help them get out of the city and had enough might to take on the [[Shining Daggers]] at the dockyard and perhaps help them with the [[Black Oars]].
- The [[Beaky Blinders]] didn't want to risk getting into an all out civil war until they truly understood who they were dealing with. Ultimately, they wanted what was best for the people of the city given the imminent threat from the invaders, and wanted [[Denarin Varn]] removed from power as his motivations were clearly not in the interest of the people.
- The Blinders wouldn't take kindly to a betrayal, and expected the party to keep to any agreement they made.
- They also warned that the [[Shining Daggers]] had given [[Denarin Varn]] their ogre as his personal bodyguard, and they suspected it was being used to guard something of significance.

After their encounter with the [[Beaky Blinders]], the party decided that this agreement was the best option they had to get their boat. They set off back to [[City Hall]] to scout it out and find a way to get inside.

Back at The Seat, the party came across the aftermath of the riot. The [[Shining Daggers]] had brutally restored control, and the crowd had now thinned. Those who remained were gathered around a makeshift funeral pyre for the young **Jorvik** who had met his demise. [[@Jack]] quietly attempted to stir up trouble again, and in the process befriended a homeless noble named **Kevin Chillbottom**. The party also saw [[Draz Steelfist]] and several [[Shining Daggers]] return from the docks and head into [[City Hall]].

Meanwhile, [[@Zharek]] took to the skies to examine [[City Hall]] from above, whilst [[@Ichoren]] explored the perimeter on foot. They discovered several possible entry points, including a side access door, windows, grated access to a basement, and most obviously of all—to those with wings anyway—a large rooftop garden on the upper floor. Here [[@Zharek]] saw a robed human male sat upon a bench. He appeared melancholic and muttering words under his breath whilst he gazed upon a large statue in the courtyard. Using his artistic skills (due to his facial injury that that prevented him from talking well), [[@Zharek]] told the party of the opportunity. Naturally, they decided the best approach was to kidnap and interrogate this person—whoever he was—and take him over to Jimmy Who's now abandoned farmstead.

[[@Zharek]] dropped from the sky and scooped up the robed man before he realised what was going on, albeit he was unexpectedly amused with his sudden magical journey through the clouds.

At Jimmy's farm, the party found out this person was a mere civil servant. However, the servant was extremely displeased with [[Denarin Varn]]'s leadership and felt it brought shame to the founder of the city, **Lord Earl Morias**, to whom he had been talking (well, to his statue). He was happy to agree to help the party if they might have a chance to help remove [[Denarin Varn]] from power. He explained:
- [[Denarin Varn]] had taken over the judge's chambers at the rear of the building, and let very few people in here. The only people he had seen going in were "undesirables". Denarin also had use of a large bedchamber on the upper floor.
- There was a rumour of a vault somewhere in the building on the upper floor, used by the previous mayor, but he had never seen it personally.
- He could probably obtain some clerk uniforms or documents to help the party infiltrate the hall in disguise. He recommended against pretending to be [[Shining Daggers]] as this was a relatively small group of mercenaries and the party were sure to be recognised, whereas there was a higher turnover of staff that might help them blend in more easily.

With that, the party head back into [[Morenthia]] having agreed to meet the servant at the [[Watchman's Rest]] after a shift of time, in the hope of enacting their cunning (half) plan...

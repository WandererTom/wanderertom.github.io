*Luminar 104 (Morning), Nordvale*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]]

The party watched as the two guards approached with their [[Um’brathir]] prisoner  bound and gagged. Having rejoined the group, [[@Jack]] immediately walked up to the prisoner and punched him in the guts to avenge the damage to his shop windows for which he thought they had obviously been responsible.

[[@Zharek]] took a more tactful approach, and using his knowledge of languages was able to talk to the prisoner, albeit the prisoner could in fact speak broken common anyway. He asked of him the [[Um’brathir]]'s purpose, and was told in no uncertain terms they sought the death of all those who inhabited these lands. The prisoner stared at him fearlessly, arrogant. [[@Ichoren]] helped question the prisoner, and the party were able to determine that [[Tu'nosdoth]] was the person [[@Zharek]] had seen watching them, and he could be found at the war camp. The prisoner laughed and told the party they would bring about their deaths sooner by seeking him out.

As the party arranged for the prisoner to be taken inside the town gates and locked up, another [[Um’brathir]] approached. He stood alone a short distance away where he confidently watched and waited. He was an older male with a distinctive golden cloak clasp, an envoy. The party approached to find out what he wanted and he eventually made his demand—the [[Um’brathir]] wanted [[The Emberglass|The Orb]]. They knew it was within [[Nordvale]], they had observed the party for some time. The party had until the Day of Long Shadows to bring it to [[Tu'nosdoth]] or they would execute their prisoners and obliterate [[Nordvale]] from the map. He also demanded the immediate return of their captured soldier. [[@Ichoren]] had already walked off, impatient and unwilling to negotiate with the enemy. [[@Zharek]] traded barbes with the envoy and dismissed his demands. The envoy had expected they might not cooperate and so revealed his hand...

Behind him two more figures emerged into view in the distance. The first was a huge soldier clad in black coloured full plate armour, with a helm fashioned in the form of a skull. To his side he held a large executioner's axe, a clear signal of his role. Next to him, bound and terrified, was [[@Splatts]]'s father. The party immediately subdued, and [[@Splatts]] cried out "that's my papa!" Feeling as if they had no choice, the party released the captured soldier. The envoy left them with a wry grin on his face and reminded them to bring [[The Emberglass|The Orb]] before it was too late. 

[[@Zharek]] took to the skies and tracked the soldiers and [[@Splatts]]'s father back to the war camp, and used his Far Sight to watch where they went. He saw the envoy entered a large tent that had its entrance flanked by two guards, and the executioner took his prisoner into a slightly smaller hexagonal shaped tent nearby. [[@Zharek]] did not follow them inside, wary that [[Tu'nosdoth]]—if he was present—might see him, even in his incorporeal form. 

The party decided their best course of action was to continue to [[Alzar's Tower]], to find out more about [[The Emberglass|The Orb]] and see if [[Uthimial]] could help them. They knew they had some time before the town was directly at risk, enough hopefully to get there and back. In the meantime, they visited [[@Jack]]'s blacksmith friend, **Tone**, a trustworthy and reliable soul, and asked him to watch over the town and keep an eye on [[Ozon Fallcrock]]. Despite his macho rivalry with [[@Ichoren]] and an arm wrestle neither of them could win, he agreed.

The party gathered supplies and equipment for their journey and left instructions for Tone and the townpeople to do the following:

- Continue to build more defences.
- Look out for Mikey returning with news of the [[Bladedancers]].
- Make sure the soldiers who were sent out the previous day returned with the explosives and stored them safely (albeit they the party had changed their mind about bombing the war camp given [[@Splatts]]' family were likely held prisoner there).
- Organise everyone who responded to [[@Splatts]]'s flyers for help to defend the town.
- Fix [[@Jack]]'s shop window (a priority for him but nobody else).

[[@Ichoren]] also took the time to visit Sammy and drop off his newly carved wooden figure.

Having decided to journey once again by river, the party set off on their sleek elven vessel. They followed the river as it wound beside the [[Nyswood]] and towards [[Omen's Deep]], where they spotted the city's high gothic towers that displayed banners and flags that marked the festival Yungentine. As they crossed Lake Omen and took the tributary towards [[Duskmire]], [[@Jack]] fished (very unsuccessfully) and [[@Ichoren]] took the opportunity to rest. 

As the evening set in, the party arrived at a small landing on the river directly south of [[Alzar's Tower]]. Here they moored but decided not to journey across the land into the hours of darkness. As the party slept, the already well-rested [[@Ichoren]] kept watch, and spotted a distance lantern that wandered across the landscape. [[@Splatts]] crept into [[@Jack]]'s room armed with a feather and an egg, and left an unsuspecting [[@Jack]] covered in yolk.

At daybreak the party set off on foot, and after a few hours came to a crossroads marked with a large wooden signpost—and on this saw four very large claw marks. [[@Jack]] investigated and used his Wolfkin hunter's instincts to determine the creature that left this mark was no ordinary creature, and it had gone off towards the [[Nyswood]] some time ago. The party decided not to investigate further.

After a few more hours of travel, the party finally arrived at [[Alzar's Tower]].

Standing before the backdrop of the [[Jagged Peaks]] was an enormous tower that was both magnificent and imposing. The tower's pale stone gleamed and shimmered faintly in the light, each stone block infused with thousands of fragments of luminescent gems. Thick, gnarled ancient vines clung to its outside and contorted themselves upwards in a spiral of green, and delicate metallic runic bands peeked and glimmered out from beneath the leaves. The tower seemed both ancient and new at same time, a juxtaposition of being. 

As the party approached closer they saw at the tower's base were several broad stone steps that led up to a large archway, set within which was a set of heavy wooden double-doors bound with silver metal. Above the entrance, carved prominently into the stone, was a symbol of a large eye set within a circle and surrounded by beams of light.

![[OrderoftheWatchfulMages.webp|100]]

The eye's pupil moved to watch as the party approached the doors. "Hello," thundered a resonant, genderless voice. 

[[@Splatts]] told the eye they had arrived to speak to [[Uthimial]].The eye cast out a bright beam of light like a spotlight, and moved this over each of the party members as they spoke. Then it put out three beams of light that spotlighted the ground before them in distinct colours. The voice spoke again, this time with a riddle:

> “Three lights I cast, yet one truth I seek—  
> Offer what is precious, fate bade you keep.  
> First, show the ember no forge could claim,  
> A wandering treasure touched by royal flame.
> 
> Next, bring the tear that knows joy from despair,  
> A gift once given to gentle a prayer.  
> Hope in a locket, sorrow undone—  
> Lifted hearts prove the worth of the one.
> 
> Last, yield the memory sealed in dark stone,  
> A spiral of elements, sun overthrown.  
> What’s lost may awaken when shadow draws near—  
> Wisdom remembered must stand without fear.
> 
> Present these three, and honest you’ll stand.  
> Hide them from sight… and be turned from this land.”

The party quickly realised the eye spoke of [[The Emberglass|The Orb]] and the [[Tear of Gamaliel]], but were stumped by the third item. That was until they realised [[@Zharek]] wore a pendant around his neck that even he had forgotten about, one that depicted a swirling sun surrounded by elements.

The party placed the items within the beams of light. The lights vanished, and then with a resonant *thoooom* the doors to [[Alzar's Tower]] opened before them...

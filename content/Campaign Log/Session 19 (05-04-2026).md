*Luminar 109 (Morning), Asantia's Blessing*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Cove-Candle]]

In a flashback, [[@Cove-Candle]] found himself in the Drunken Dwarf in [[Duskmire]] as night set in. The proprietor directed him to Martin's room, and he ventured up to speak with him.

Martin, a young human male, greeting [[@Cove-Candle]] cautiously, but invited him into his room to speak. [[@Cove-Candle]] explained he'd heard Martin had memory issues and explained he had the same. He'd also heard stories of a Niad. Martin explained he'd washed up on the shore recently and couldn't even remember his own name or anything about himself. However, every night he had the same vivid dream: He sailed through the mist on his boat and a wonderous island appeared before him. He went ashore, where he heard the song of a woman drawing him closer. Eventually he found her, an entrancingly beautiful person wearing a wonderous necklace set with a large pearl in the centre. She was ever-so kind to him, welcoming. He knew he loved her dearly. But his dreams never showed him leaving.

[[@Cove-Candle]] made out he had similar dreams too, and was curious to hear about Martin's story. He promised to return one day to collect Martin and they could go out and seek this Niad - or whatever she was - together. This was something they both wanted to do.

Afterwards, [[@Cove-Candle]] discussed what Martin had told him with the group. They wondered if the Isle of Tharos could be this island - perhaps sailors know it is there but never remember returning from it. 

Back in the timeline, the party - or the Splatt Pack as they had decided to call themselves (to Jack's dismay) - took in the view of [[Asantia's Blessing]].

Deciding to take the most direct route through, they travelled west, passing through the ruins of an ancient city on the way. They rummaged through the buildings, finding the odd coin and gemstone, despite [[@Splatts]] nearly falling through the floor.

After camping for the night, they set out the next day taking to the hills. It wasn't long before they found themselves hunted - two huge worgs stalked them. Soon the beasts charged in. [[@Jack]] managed to growl and dissuade one from attacking immediately. [[@Zharek]] brought lightning down from the heavens in a spectacular display of his new magic, striking the other, although had rather underwhelming impact. Unfortunately for [[@Splatts]], he was soon caught in one's foul jaws and dragged across the ground. [[@Cove-Candle]] charged into the other, carving into it with uncharacteristic skill with his blade. [[@Ichoren]] managed to save [[@Splatts]] from further trouble by rushing over and taking out the beast with a well thrown axe. Then [[@Cove-Candle]] finished the other.

After taking some time to heal up, the party continued on and came across the ruins of a small town. In the distance [[@Zharek]] spotted a creature ahead, using Farsight to reveal a centaur. They decided to approach rather than to avoid him. 

Attempting his charm (and very nearly failing), [[@Jack]] ran up to the centaur and introduced himself. Armed with a spear, and taking a guard-like pose, **HobNob** questioned the party's presence at first. However, he soon came around to the idea of having some company for the evening, largely aided by [[@Splatts]] kind words. He invited them in for a meal and a rest in his home for the night. He told them he'd been guarding this town for centuries, the rest of his kin having fallen long ago. It was his last order, and he would remain until relieved of his duty.

As they parted, [[@Splatts]] gave HobNob a carved centaur he'd asked [[@Ichoren]] to fashion during the night. HobHob placed it reverently on his mantle, overwhelmed by the generosity and the delightfulness of his gift. In return, he gifted the party some of his own possessions:
- [[@Splatts]] a small shield emblazoned with the image of a centaur,
- [[@Jack]] 25 silver pieces,
- [[@Cove-Candle]] 110 silver pieces,
- [[@Zharek]] a mysterious potion bottle that may or may not help with healing, and
- [[@Ichoren]] a flute.

He waved the party off into the distance as they made their way deeper into [[Asantia's Blessing]] despite his warnings...

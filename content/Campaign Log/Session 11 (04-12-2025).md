*Luminar 103 (Morning), The Evervein*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Zharek]]

Having escaped [[Morenthia]] and the clutches of [[Denarin Varn]] and the [[Black Oars]], the party journeyed down the [[Evervein]] on [[Cove-Candle's Sloop]]. The journey was peaceful and pleasant, a stark change from the chaos of the city. With the warm Luminar sun on their backs, they travelled for miles as the sloop cut swiftly through the water, its sleek elven design making barely a whisper.

After a time, the party realised how disconcertingly quiet it was on the river. The usual thoroughfare of waterborne trade and passage was no longer there. The river was empty, save for the occasional heron and wildlife that curiously watched as they passed by.

As the party reached the midway point between [[Morenthia]] and [[Nordvale]], the river rounded into a long, wide bend that was flanked by weeds and vegetation. Here, the air cooled noticeably and the party found themselves in a patch of thick mist that had rolled across the land.

[[@Ichoren]] kept watch at the front of the ship but struggled to see clearly through the mist. His focus was distracted by the continuous sound of retching from [[@Zharek]] and [[@Splatts]] whose constitutions had not agreed with travel on the water. [[@Splatts]] was despondent and felt he brought shame on all mallards for his lack of seafaring ability.

Suddenly the ship shuddered, and a loud bang signaled they had collided with something in the water just off the starboard side. [[@Ichoren]] peered over and saw the wreckage of a small fishing vessel that floated in pieces in the mist-covered water. He realised the damage to that boat had not been caused by them. Then he saw a small rowing boat bobbed nearby, and sat inside was a soggy and very fearful looking fisherman. The fisherman peered up at [[@Ichoren]] with wide eyes and immediately gestured at him to be quiet. 

[[@Ichoren]] threw out a rope to the fisherman—Trevor—who quietly pulled himself in. "*Shhhhhhhhhhhhhh, it will hear you!*" he said, anxious. Then [[@Ichoren]] noticed a dark shape moving beneath the waves.

[[@Zharek]] cast Far Sight to place himself under the water, and in doing so was able to see what lurked beneath—a huge, long, winding sea serpent. 

The party, very carefully and quietly, hoisted the sails and attempted to coast along the river in silence, hoping to be left alone. [[@Zharek]] spotted the wreckage of another ship ahead, in a direct collision course with them. He warned [[@Splatts]] who attempted to steer their vessel, but it was too little too late. Although they did not impact the wreck head-on, they heard an almighty scraping sound as the debris grinded against the hull of their ship. 

Behind Trevor, the huge, cylindrical form of the serpent rose from the depths. It plucked Trevor from the deck in its jaws and gobbled him up. The party watched in horror as he disappeared into the creature head first. The fight was on.

The serpent attacked fast and viciously, and struck out at [[@Ichoren]] plunging its fangs into his arm. [[@Zharek]] cast Flick to strike it in the eye, and although this caused minimal damage he managed to temporarily impede it. [[@Splatts]] set to work and aimed one of the cannons, and before long the party managed to fire off a shot that ripped through the creature in a blast of gore.

The serpent struck back with vengeance. As [[@Zharek]] dodged one of its tail strikes, it bit out again at [[@Ichoren]] and this time managed to consume him fully. As [[@Ichoren]] plunged into the beast he whipped out his axe ready to tear it apart from the inside. The creature caught [[@Splatts]] in its tail and dragged him overboard by his feet and deep down under the water.

[[@Zharek]] suddenly found himself alone on the deck of the ship, his party either devoured or drowning. Keeping his composure, he managed to fire off another cannon shot and ripped a huge hole in the beast's belly. Then he charged up his Power Fist, picked up a nearby cannon ball, flew up to the beast's head, and brutally slammed his reinforced fist  through its skull to finally slay it. As the beast's body went limp, [[@Ichoren]] and [[@Splatts]] freed themselves. [[@Ichoren]] dove back into the serpent's belly to rescue the very grateful and slime-covered Trevor.

As the party collected themselves and [[@Splatts]] applied some much needed bandages to several of them, they noticed the distant silhouette of a figure in the mist. Someone stood atop a hillside close to the riverbank and watched them from a distance. [[@Zharek]] once against projected his presence using Far Sight to observe the figure close up. He saw a large, muscular male who stood about 7ft tall and had deep red coloured skin. He wore a long, black hooded cloak, beneath which glinted a pair of deep black eyes that watched the party intently. His face was hairless, sharp, emotionless. To his side, held out like a staff, he carried a large glaive topped with a vicious wave-like blade—serrated on one side and brought to a sharp point on the other. Tattooed on the centre of his forehead was a large, distinctive symbol.  

![[Tunosdoth Symbol.png|100]]  
Suddenly the figure looked directly at [[@Zharek]]—not to his physical location, but directly at [[@Zharek]]'s magical projection, as if meeting his eyes. He slowly lowered his glaive so the blade was level and pointed it directly at [[@Zharek]]'s projection. He smiled a frightening, knowing smile, and revealed a set of sharpened triangular teeth. Then he turned, strode down the hill, and vanished into the mist.

[[@Zharek]] relayed what he had seen to the party, and they realised this must have been some kind of important or powerful [[Um’brathir]]. They worried what his presence meant. 

After this unsettling encounter, the party got some rest as they continued on their journey to [[Nordvale]]. 

[[@Splatts]] leaned against the side of the boat and fell into a short, restless sleep. He dreamt of a sudden, extreme warmth that emanated from the sack that contained [[The Emberglass|The Orb]]. When he opened it, the red light from inside was blinding. The world around him faded, and he found himself enveloped in a warm red glow. Then he heard a female voice, softly and kind, but with an edge of firmness...

*"You have done well, Splatts. Keep on this path and more will be revealed to you. Make haste, do not delay, that fate of us all depends on it."*

Then his vision changed, and he found himself rushing over the land like he was a bird soaring above, until he reached a huge tower that rose high up into the clouds, set against a backdrop of harsh, angular mountains. The vision zoomed in further to a symbol on the tower, an eye surrounded by bright rays of sunlight. [[@Splatts]]' head jerked as he woke up.

[[@Splatts]] splashed some water on his face and told [[@Ichoren]] of his dream. [[@Zharek]] watched from the crow's nest as [[@Splatts]] realised the sack that contained the orb was warm. As he opened it up, the red light inside faded and the temperature cooled. 

As evening set in, the party saw the jetties of [[Nordvale]]'s dock come into view. Stood upon the dock were several figures—locals dressed in makeshift, ill-fitting armour. They lowered crossbows at the party with shaky hands. "Identify yourselves," one called.

[[@Splatts]] and [[@Ichoren]] (and Trevor) quickly settled the nerves of the guards because they recognised several of them from the town. [[@Ichoren]] spoke to "Old Man" Ronald, who told him that there had been all kinds of trouble and [[Ozon Fallcrock]] was in charge of the town's defences. He was based in the Town Hall. 

On their way to the Town Hall, the party saw how the usual bustling and lively town was now quiet and fearful. People walked around in pairs, the noisy trade had stopped, and parents kept their children indoors. One child—little Samuel—broke from his mother's grip and ran up to [[@Ichoren]] and pleaded for him to repair his broken wooden toy figure. [[@Ichoren]] struggled, but promised to make him another.

At the Town Hall, the party spoke with [[Ozon Fallcrock]]. He explained that all the [[Valeguard]] had left to join the war effort, and they only had ordinary townspeople left to defend themselves. He asked what the party knew about the invasion and recent events, and relayed what information he had:
- They had sent several scouts out from [[Nordvale]] in the last couple of days but only two had returned. One scout reported the invaders had started to establish a war camp about 10km directly east of [[Nordvale]] on the edge of the [[Vale of Songs]].
- They'd heard reports that most of the west coast had fallen, including major cities such as Lightfall. The invaders had pushed through several towns and villages on their way east, including [[Valesview]].
- The other towns nearby were likely in the same position as [[Nordvale]], as all the [[Valeguard]] had been called up to fight on the front lines.
- Most disconcertingly, no-one seemed to have seen or heard from [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] since the attack on [[Vadoria City]]. The nation seemed to be leaderless, and no orders or guidance had arrived from up high.
- The invaders sent small raiding parties the previous day to seemingly test [[Nordvale]]'s defences, and they anticipated a larger assault was imminent.
- One of the guards—Simeon—saw suspicious hooded figures creeping about the town overnight. They feared the town had been infiltrated and there was possible sabotage, perhaps in preparation for what was to come.

[[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]] exclaimed the only logical course of action was to surrender to the invaders. They were outmatched and could not fight back. He felt assembling the defences was a lost cause, and it was better to plead for their lives and hopefully be spared. [[@Ichoren]] vehemently disagreed and pulled rank (as he was now a Count) to take charge.

The party spoke to another guard—Mikey—who expressed he didn't want to surrender. He wasn't sure of [[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]]'s intentions, and whether he had just given up hope or had been corrupted. He told the party the whole town was scared, and a farmer and his family had been found executed just outside the town the previous day. [[@Ichoren]] asked him to gather what weapons and equipment he could, and [[@Zharek]] asked him to find out where he could obtain some material to make explosives.

As [[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]] left the hall, [[@Zharek]] took to the skies to discreetly follow him. The party were suspicious, and wanted to find out more about his intentions.

[[@Zharek]] watched as [[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]] walked down the high street towards the docks, before he turned into an alleyway. He looked back over his shoulders as he walked, seemingly wary. He then disappeared into a nearby house.

[[@Zharek]] took up a position on a high rooftop and—perched like a gargoyle—waited to see what happened next...

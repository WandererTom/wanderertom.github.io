*Luminar 114 (Afternoon), Veilhold*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]]

The portal opened into a dark, dank chamber. [[@Jack]] lit a torch, revealing they were in some kind of crypt. Stone steps ascended to the surface and the wasted no time in following them upwards.

As they emerged above the ground, the party heard the sound of humming from beyond. [[@Ichoren]] peeked out to assess their surroundings and saw a solitary old man tending to gravestones. They were in the middle of a graveyard. Despite being mid afternoon, a thick mist that swirled all around obscurung the day's light. Like a permanently low cloud, it chilled the air and cast perpetual twilight. 

Nervous of their gloomy surroundings, but keen to progress, the party approached the old man who greeted them warmly. He called himself **Mr Moon**, and asked if they had come to help him clean up. They spoke to him for a short while and learned they were now in [[Veilhold]]. He told them yhe town was small and rundown, and dominated by a great mansion that was now used by [[Church of Velthira|The Church]]. The party could make out the top of the bell tower above the mists a short walk away. All of the island's residents were members of [[Church of Velthira|The Church]].

Mr Moon told them [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] was led by [[Amelia Darkscale]], Exulted Priestess of Shadow. When [[@Ichoren]] asked if they used any specific religious texts, he said **Brenda**, the Holy Keeper of Devotion, might be able to tell him more.

The party decided to head into town to find [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] to learn more about what was going on. They wondered if [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] somehow controlled the mists with magic.

They took a path towards the clifftops, finding the town of [[Veilhold]] lay there with a view beyond over the watery expanse of the [[Weeping Deep]]. They crossed a point where the path split and dropped down to the cliffs below. At the foot of the cliffs they made out the top of a large building shrouded in especially thick mist.

[[@Zharek]] wondered about the mist. It seemed unnatural and he noticed a strange and subtle chemical-like smell to it.

Having taken the path towards the town, the party made their way through the eerie buildings and soon arrived at a large gothic-style mansion surrounded by wrought iron gates. Carved over the large main entranceway was a sign reading, "The Chruch of Velthira". [[@Jack]] recalled what they'd learned about [[Velthira]], and that she was the godess of shadow and secrets. They also knew one of [[The Twelve]] had been here. They wasted no time and entered the building through the main doors.

Inside they were greeted by a slender cat-person of sorts, who welcomed them to the abode of [[Church of Velthira|The Church]]. He asked if they had come to join them and [[@Jack]] explained he was a historian and he and his companions had come to learn about their ways. 

Soon [[Amelia Darkscale]] presented herself - a lizard-person clad in a long purple robe. She sauntered down the staircase and greeted them warmly. She said she was overjoyed to have guests, and insisted they should stay to enjoy a meal with them and rest from their travels. Despite her polite greeting, she managed to offend [[@Ichoren]] who took great exception to being judged by his physicality and not his intellect.

The party were taken to a lounge area where they waited. As they examined the room they saw the mansion was a shadow of its former glory - clearly once grand and ostentatious, but now old and rundown.
- [[@Zharek]] looked through the bookshelves and found various tomes about magic, albeit no spells.
- [[@Splatts]] found a chronical about [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] that spoke of their worship of **The Deep One** in service to [[Velthira]].
- The party also discovered a calendar containing a few entries:

> Luminar 102 - Brenda's Birthday
> Luminar 105 - Annual General Meeting
> Luminar 111 - Quiz Night
> Luminar 115 - Sacrifice an underling to The Deep One
> Luminar 116 - Book Club
> Luminar 120 - Meeting with Lorus about mist quality

- [[@Jack]] stared at himself in a large mirror and, for a brief moment, saw a pair of eyes that were not his own staring back.

After a while the party were ushered into an adjoining dining chamber and presented with a fine spread of many foods. [[Amelia Darkscale]] placed herself at the head of the table, and [[@Ichoren]] took the spot at the opposite end. Several other members of [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] gathered, and the party were encouraged to sit. After [[@Jack]], upon request, opened the meal with a prayer, they were invited to enjoy the meal before them. Most of the party were by now highly suspicious, except [[@Jack]] who immediately tore into a roasted chicken and ate with vigor.

Stood nearby was a young servant girl, **Min**. She presented the guests with drinks, but [[@Ichoren]] caught a very subtle warning from her not to consume them. She appeared terrified.

During the meal, [[@Splatts]] pushed his food around his plate and made small-talk. [[@Ichoren]] ate nothing at all, feigning continued offence at his greeting earlier and claiming lack of appetite. [[@Zharek]] took small bites of his meal, mainly following pressure from the congregant sat beside him who told him failing to partake would risk offending their generous host.

As they feasted, or avoided feasting, [[Amelia Darkscale]] spoke with them at length, happily encouraging them to learn more about [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] and join them.

After a while, [[@Ichoren]] continued to feign offence and left the meal. [[@Zharek]] and [[@Splatts]] seized the excuse to follow him, and all three were shown to bed chambers with the offer of rest for the night. [[@Jack]] stayed behind and continued to eat heartily as he conversed with [[Amelia Darkscale]]. When asked, she told him about **The Deep One**, explaining they beleived it was the physical embodiment of [[Velthira]]. The being was released long ago when great magics tore the land apart. She asked if he would like to be blessed by **The Deep One**, and she promised an eventful day would follow the night. 

Eventually, [[@Jack]] retired to his chamber. During the night, [[@Ichoren]] snuck downstairs in an effort to find **Min**. He found her tucked in the corner of a room accessed from the dining hall, and quietly woke her to find out what was going on. While she did not speak a word, she indicated they were all in danger and should run.

Hearing a noise nearby, [[@Ichoren]] hid in the shadows just in time to watchas [[Amelia Darkscale]] entered the room. In contrast to her warm, friendly character before, she coldly chastised **Min** and accused her of leaving the room. She threatened **Min** that if she was caught she would follow the same path as her brother - whatever that was. 

After [[Amelia Darkscale]] had left, [[@Ichoren]] promised to help **Min** escape. 

In the light of the new day, [[@Ichoren]] made his way to [[@Splatts]]' room. Fearing eavesdroppers, he drew a picture to explain what he'd discovered in the night. Then they both went to find [[@Zharek]], only to discover his room was now empty and devoid of belongings. [[@Jack]]'s chamber was the same.

Meanwhile, somewhere dark, damp, and foul smelling, [[@Jack]] woke up. He found himself bound and gagged, and saw beside him lay the unconscious [[@Zharek]]...

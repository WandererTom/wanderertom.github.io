*Luminar 114 (Afternoon), Veilhold*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]]

The portal opened into a dark, dank chamber. [[@Jack]] lit a torch, revealing they were in some kind of crypt. Stone steps ascended to the surface and the party wasted no time in following them upwards.

As they emerged above ground, they heard the sound of someone humming nearby. [[@Ichoren]] peeked out to assess their surroundings. They were in the middle of a graveyard, occupied by a solitary old man who tended to the gravestones. As he looked around, [[@Ichoren]] saw there was a thick mist in the air all around. It obscured much of his view of their surroundings, but he could make out a large bell tower that loomed above it in the distance. The mist hung like a low dense cloud, chilling the air and casting the land in perpetual twilight.

Nervous of their gloomy surroundings but keen to progress onwards, the party carefully approached the old man. Thankfully, he greeted them warmly. He called himself **Mr Moon**, and asked if they had come to help him clean up. They spoke to him for a short while and learned they had arrived in [[Veilhold]]. He told them the town was small and rundown, and dominated by the great mansion that was now home to [[Church of Velthira|The Church]]. The bell tower marked its location a short walk away. He told them all of the island's residents were members of [[Church of Velthira|The Church]].

After questioning the old man further, the party learned [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] was led by [[Amelia Darkscale]], Exulted Priestess of Shadow. When [[@Ichoren]] asked if they based their religion any specific religious texts, Mr Moon directed him to **Brenda**, the Holy Keeper of Devotion, who might be able to tell him more.

The party decided to head into town to find [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] and learn more about what was going on. They wondered if [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] somehow controlled the mist with magic.

They took a path that headed towards the tower. Soon the town of [[Veilhold]] emerged from the mist, and beyond a great view over the expanse of the [[Weeping Deep]]. At one point the path split, with one route dropping down to the cliffs below. Down at the foot of the cliffs they could make out the top of a large building shrouded in especially thick mist.

[[@Zharek]] wondered about the mist. He thought it seemed unnatural and he noticed a strange and subtle chemical-like smell to it.

The party continued on the path towards the town, making their way through the eerie buildings. They soon arrived at a large gothic-style mansion surrounded by wrought iron gates. Carved over the large main entranceway they saw a sign that read, "The Chruch of Velthira". [[@Jack]] recalled what they'd learned about [[Velthira]] recently, that she was the godess of shadow and secrets. The party also recalled one of [[The Twelve]] had been here before. They entered the building through the main doors, finding them unlocked.

Inside they were greeted by a slender cat-person of sorts, who welcomed them to the home of [[Church of Velthira|The Church]]. He asked if they had come to join them and [[@Jack]] explained he was a historian and he and his companions had come to learn about [[Church of Velthira|The Church]]'s ways. 

Then [[Amelia Darkscale]] presented herself. She was a tall and slender a lizard-person clad in a long purple robe. She sauntered her way down the staircase and greeted them warmly. She was overjoyed to have guests, and insisted they should stay to enjoy a meal and rest from their travels. Despite her polite greeting, she managed to offend [[@Ichoren]] who took great exception to being judged by his physicality and not his intellect.

The party were led into a drawing room to wait. As they examined the room they noticed the mansion was a shadow of its former glory - it was clearly once grand and ostentatious, but now old and rundown.
- [[@Zharek]] looked through the bookshelves and found various tomes about magic, albeit no spells.
- [[@Splatts]] found a chronical about [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] that spoke of their worship of **The Deep One** in service to [[Velthira]].
- The party also discovered a calendar containing a few entries:

> Luminar 102 - Brenda's Birthday
> Luminar 105 - Annual General Meeting
> Luminar 111 - Quiz Night
> Luminar 115 - Sacrifice an underling to The Deep One
> Luminar 116 - Book Club
> Luminar 120 - Meeting with Lorus about mist quality

- [[@Jack]] stared at himself in a large mirror and, for a brief moment, saw a pair of eyes that were not his own staring back.

After a while the party were ushered into an adjoining dining chamber where they were presented with a very fine spread of many foods. [[Amelia Darkscale]] placed herself at the head of the table, and [[@Ichoren]] took the spot at the opposite end. Several other members of [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] gathered, and the party took their seats. [[@Jack]], upon request, opened with a prayer, and they were invited to enjoy the meal before them. Most of the party were by now highly suspicious, except [[@Jack]] who immediately tore into a roast chicken and ate with vigor.

Stood nearby was a young servant girl named **Min**. She presented the guests with drinks, but [[@Ichoren]] caught a very subtle warning from her - a careful shake of her head. She appeared terrified.

[[@Splatts]] pushed his food around his plate and made small-talk. [[@Ichoren]] ate nothing at all, feigning continued offence at his greeting earlier and claiming lack of appetite. [[@Zharek]] took small bites of his meal, mainly because of pressure from the congregant sat beside him who told him failing to partake risked offending their generous host.

As they feasted, or avoided feasting, [[Amelia Darkscale]] spoke with them at length, happily encouraging them to learn more about [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] and join their ranks.

After a while, [[@Ichoren]] continued to feign offence and left the dining hall. [[@Zharek]] and [[@Splatts]] seized the excuse to follow him, and all three were shown to bed chambers with the offer of resting there for the night. [[@Jack]] stayed behind and continued to eat heartily as he conversed with [[Amelia Darkscale]]. When asked, she told him about **The Deep One**, explaining they beleived it was the physical embodiment of [[Velthira]]. It was released long ago when great magics tore the land apart. She asked if he would like to be blessed by **The Deep One**, and she promised the next day would be truely eventful. 

Eventually, [[@Jack]] retired to his chamber. During the night, [[@Ichoren]] snuck downstairs in an effort to find **Min**. He found her tucked in the corner of a room near to the dining hall, and quietly woke her hoping to find out what was going on. She did not speak a word, but nodded to indicat they were all in danger and should run.

Hearing a noise nearby, [[@Ichoren]] hid in the shadows just before [[Amelia Darkscale]] entered the room. In contrast to her warm, friendly character before, she coldly chastised **Min** and accused her of leaving the room. She threatened **Min** that if she was caught out of the room she would follow the same path as her brother.

After [[Amelia Darkscale]] left, [[@Ichoren]] promised to help **Min** escape. 

In the light of the new day, [[@Ichoren]] made his way to [[@Splatts]]' room. Fearing eavesdroppers, he drew a picture to explain what he'd discovered in the night. Then they both went to find [[@Zharek]], only to discover his room was empty and devoid of belongings. [[@Jack]]'s chamber was the same. Confused, they wondered where they were. 

Meanwhile, [[@Jack]] opened his groggy eyes. He was somewhere dark, damp, and terribly foul smelling. He found he was bound and gagged, and saw [[@Zharek]] lay beside him unconscious...

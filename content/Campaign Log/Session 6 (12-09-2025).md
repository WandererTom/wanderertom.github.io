*Luminar 101 (Afternoon), Morenthia*  
[[@Jack]], [[@Ichoren]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Cove-Candle]], [[@Kirou]]

As the party approached the western gate of [[Morenthia]], they heard the sound of hoofs from behind them. They saw a member of [[Lord Ronald Squinketon|Lord Squinketon]]'s household guard who brought with him mounted on his steed [[@Cove-Candle]] and [[@Kirou]]. He dropped them with the party, berated [[@Jack]] for not looking after his son, and rode off again.

The reunited group took some time to catch up with recent events and [[@Ichoren]] gave [[@Cove-Candle]] the letter from [[Lord Ronald Squinketon|Lord Squinketon]] that contained promise of his boat. [[@Cove-Candle]] and [[@Kirou]] were introduced to [[Lady Elira of House Vaelin|Lady Elira]] and [[Tibbin (Tibs) Figleaf|Tibs]], with [[@Kirou]] seemingly finding favour with both. The group then continued their walk to the nearby gate of [[Morenthia]].

Here they found a throng of people queuing to enter the city as guards watched over them nervously. The party followed [[Lady Elira of House Vaelin|Lady Elira]]'s lead and head directly to the front of the queue, despite  discontent from the crowd and [[@Ichoren]]'s attempt to join the back of the queue. The guard at the head of the queue immediately recognised [[Lady Elira of House Vaelin|Lady Elira]] and granted her entry, and in doing so bowed respectfully and welcomed her home. He was not so friendly with the rest of the party, but [[@Jack]] managed to persuade him (and [[Lady Elira of House Vaelin|Lady Elira]]) that they were in her company and they so all were begrudgingly allowed access.

The party were, however, compelled to register their arrival and submit to being searched by several waiting guards, including a rather large and enthusiastic female guard - Big Gertha. Unfortunately for [[@Jack]], during this uncomfortable process he became increasingly unwell and found himself dashing to the nearest privy.

[[@Splatts]] was concerned about the guard finding [[The Emberglass]], but managed to appeal to Gertha's interest in weightlifting and encouraged her to lift a large statue to demonstrate her strength. This distracted the guards from the task at hand, and ultimately allowed both [[@Splatts]] and [[@Kirou]] to enter without being searched.

[[@Kirou]] was not completely unnoticed by all, for a young child in the crowd waved furiously at him, and believed his name to be "Vayne Farhunter". [[@Kirou]] did not recognise the child, Olan, despite his claims to have grown up with him in [[Isle of Arcantia|Arcantia]].

Once [[@Jack]] had recovered, the party entered Morenthia proper. From the western gate they looked out into a large marketplace, once full of goods and stalls, now emptied and replaced with makeshift tents for refugees. [[@Jack]] listened to a group of refugees who were stood gathered around a female on a makeshift platform, and who expressed discontent at how the refugees were being treated following their arrival. In the houses around the market many of the residents packed up their belongings, in the process of fleeing the city. The nearby [[Valeguard]] headquarters was all but abandoned, with the odd injured guard slumped outside. The party noticed all the other guards - those not wearing uniforms of the [[Valeguard]] - wore cloaks fastened with a silver pin in the shape of a dagger.

Intending to head towards the docks to collect [[@Cove-Candle]]'s promised boat, the party passed by the [[Watchman's Rest]] where they heard sounds of a ruckus. A fight spilled out into the street before them, but was calmed by a man claiming to have heard stories from [[Valesview]] of a resistance - a group had fought back against the invaders. It did not take the party long to inform the crowd they were the group responsible for these heroic acts, and they basked in the praise and adoration of many. [[@Zharek]] "Justzarack", member of the now esteemed resistance group "TBC", inadvertently encouraged the group to believe they were the saviors of [[Morenthia]].

Taking advantage of their new and unexpectedly lofty status, the party went into the tavern for some much needed rest. Gifted free board, most of the party retired upstairs to their chambers, leaving [[@Ichoren]] and [[@Splatts]] downstairs. [[@Ichoren]] took the opportunity to ask the proprietor about [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]]...

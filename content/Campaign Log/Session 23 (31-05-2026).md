*Luminar 113 (Afternoon), Nethirial*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]]

Before heading over to the [[Palace of Nethirial]], the party decided to leave some of their equipment in their suite at the **Hairy Wolfkin**. [[@Zharek]] didn't want to risk taking the [[Dawnmother's Diadem]] into the palace in case it was discovered, but the party decided they should take [[The Emberglass]] so they could hopefully find the [[Emperor of Nethiria]] and present it to him.

[[@Zharek]] found a hiding place for the diadem, and [[@Jack]] hurriedly removed his copies of *Furry Bits* magazine from the same spot to make space.

Then the party made their way to the palace. They presented the guards with their written authorisation and proof of employment, and were granted entry. In the kitchen they found the head cook, **Henrietta**, and a young kitchen boy named **Isaac**. Henrietta immediately put them to work, and they soon found themselves delivering a meal to [[Xinia]] in the throne chamber.

They found [[Xinia]] in the long chamber sat upon a lower throne reserved for the [[Xinia|Hand of the Emperor]]. The party stood aside so as to not attract attention and waited, seeing that two guards were presenting a prisoner before the elven Hand. He was an elderly beggar who was accused of peddling rumours that [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]] was actually imprisoned within the palace. After hearing of his alleged crimes, [[Xinia]] nodded to a nearby heavily armoured soldier who unsheathed a long blade and promptly executed the man on the spot. As if the incident was trivial, [[Xinia]] then turned her attention to the nearby waiting servants and her meal.

She beckoned the party over, who cautiously stepped around the pooling blood and brought over her meal of sardines, bread, and a special jam that [[@Zharek]] had made up. Fortunately, she was pleased with her meal - especially the jam, offering it to one of her trusted advisors to try.

[[@Jack]] seized the opportunity and potential good favour to ask [[Xinia]] about the rumours the man had been accused of spreading. She did not take kindly to this line of questioning, nor to servants listening to the going's on of the court. The party witnessed her anger rise and with it all light faded from the room. With a booming, unnatural voice, she commanded them not to speak of these rumours and to make it known that [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]] ruled absolute - and those who questioned this fact risked death. The party hastily made their way out of the throne room as soon as they got the opportunity.

Afforded a short break from working, the party explored the palace. They quickly found a secret passage into [[Xinia]]'s chamber, and [[@Jack]] picked the locks of a desk drawer to find two documents of note:
- A schedule recording planned changeovers of the guards and mealtimes relating to a special prison "for the Emperor".
- A page of ramblings about the dark arts and having power over death.

[[@Jack]] snuck into an adjoining room, finding a large bedchamber containing fine furniture and a bed adorned with fine silk sheets. He liberated six silver pieces from a bedside table.

Back outside the chamber, the party found Isaac and [[@Jack]] bribed him for information, paying him the six silver pieces he'd just stolen from [[Xinia]] (albeit did not tell Isaac that). In exchange, Isaac told them how he delivered meals to the guards who stood watch in front of the steps in the centre of the palace that led to the upper floor. He's never been up there himself, but had heard rumours [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]] was imprisoned somewhere up there, so he liked to make sure there was a good apple with each of the meals for him. He'd also heard [[Xinia]] had been seen disappearing into a bookshelf by some of the servants.

As they explored further, [[@Zharek]] came across several guards in training. He sparred well with the head guard, **Aela**, and earned himself an invite to join the guard service should he choose. 

The party also found two locked doors in the western wing but chose not to try and enter them.

The party returned to work for the evening, and after a while [[@Jack]] and [[@Splatts]] once again found themselves delivering a meal to [[Xinia]]. The throne room was by now much emptier, and she stood in quiet conversation with two advisors. Seeing the two servants enter, and seemingly curious having noticed they were new, [[Xinia]] invited [[@Jack]] forwards to speak with her. She commended his bravery for speaking up earlier - something few do, and even less succeed at. She asked him directly why he was truly there. He told her he sought to serve [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]], and he asked his question because he was so deeply concerned about the terrible lies that had been spread by the prisoner about him. Unfortunately for [[@Jack]], [[Xinia]] did not believe him in the slightest, and stood above him as he knelt, pointing a blade to his throat. She accused him of being a spy, and her wrath once against rose and charged the room with a terrible, oppressive energy. [[@Splatts]] interjected just in time, and somehow convinced her that [[@Jack]] had really been desperate to serve her because he was so infatuated with her. At this, [[Xinia]] softened noticeably, seemingly flattered by the attention. Her demeanor changed entirely, and softened to her usual graceful tone. She asked [[@Jack]] again what he was really doing there, and he confirmed [[@Splatts]] spoke the truth. Then she asked him if he was ambitious, and what it was that he truly sought. [[@Jack]] responded that he wanted to serve her, but above all he wanted riches. She asked him if he wanted to crush the common folk, to rise above them - to be better than they were. He confirmed he did, without hesitation. She appeared to admire both his candor and his ambition. Quite suddenly and unexpectedly, [[@Jack]] found himself promoted - he would serve as one of her personal servants. She told him if he served her well, she would reward his loyalty with the riches he sought. His first task, however, was to escort her to her bedchambers. As he accompanied her inside, she spoke instructions to her advisors that the pair should not be disturbed for the night...

*Luminar 108 (Morning), Duskmire*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]]

In the new day's light, the party breakfasted in the **Drunken Dwarf**. [[@Ichoren]] was quiet and detached. [[@Splatts]] made him a nice breakfast and asked how he was, noticing he wasn't himself. [[@Ichoren]] immediately broke down and started weeping. He said he hadn't wanted to kill the scientist, but felt he'd had no other choice - he would have told [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]] about [[@Ichoren]] and the party if he'd let him go. As [[@Splatts]] consoled him, [[@Jack]] came down to see [[@Ichoren]] crying, and immediately swerved him to avoid any emotional engagement.

Afterwards, the party made their way to their boat and set sail, intending to travel south and hug the coastline until they neared [[Ombrin's Beacon]]. [[@Jack]] noticed a fisher-folk waved furiously at them from the shoreline - he waved back in response until the man was a mere speck in the distance. 

The waters of the [[Weeping Deep]] were deathly still. The huge blanket of thick fog obscured any view of the waters to their east. They continued south, a slow journey only made possible by [[@Cove-Candle]]'s expert seamanship and his ability to capture what little wind was present in the sails. Eventually they saw the great mountain peak emerge from the mist to their south, and steered the vessel into a cove as close to [[Ombrin's Beacon]] as they could get. Here they anchored the ship and [[@Ichoren]] removed anything that might reveal who had used it, worried it might be discovered by others. They rowed to shore, noticing the unusually cold waters and faint outlines of icebergs in the misty waters further south.

The walk to [[Ombrin's Beacon]] was uneventful and made relatively easy by the flat grasslands of [[The Edgelands]]. As they arrived at the small mountain, they saw a permanent camp was carved into the south-east peak above. [[@Zharek]] assessed it using Farsight, seeing it contained only a few remaining soldiers who were all wearing grey cloaks and the emblems of [[The Bladedancers]] (a sword swirling in a circle). The rest of the soldiers, it appeared, had already left the camp.

They talked their way past a guard and met the leader of [[The Bladedancers]], a dwarf named [[Brin Leafkin]]. They were able to convince him, largely aided by [[Lord Timbrel Toodlepip|Lord Toodlepip]]'s signet ring, that they had come to seek their help. [[Brin Leafkin]] was already aware of the trouble in [[Nordvale]] and the invasion, having heard from the little bird [[Jeremy]]. He called [[Jeremy]] over, who fluttered own and was very pleased to see [[@Zharek]] again. [[@Zharek]] in turn rewarded him with the promised pickled onions.

[[Brin Leafkin]] explained he'd already sent most of the [[Bladedancers]] north to aid [[Nordvale]]. The party were able to provide valuable intelligence (despite [[@Zharek]]'s dodgy drawing skills) and [[@Splatts]] told him all about his captured family. In private, the party told him more - about their mission with [[The Emberglass]] and their need to get into [[Asantia's Blessing]]. [[Brin Leafkin]] said no person had entered into [[Asantia's Blessing]] during his lifetime - and in fact he was sworn to help ensure that remained the case - but having heard the truth from them offered to aid them however he could. At the very least, he would not interfere.

Upon parting, [[Brin Leafkin]] promised to look out for Mikey on their travels to [[Nordvale]] and not knowingly do anything to jeopardise the safety of [[@Splatts]]'s family.

During the walk to [[Edengate]], the party made camp along the way. Whilst the others slept in the tent, [[@Ichoren]] and [[Aetheren Clay|Aetheren]] spoke of several things including her awakening. She thanked [[@Ichoren]] for what he had done. Overwhelmed by the world, she asked [[@Ichoren]] how he had managed to cope, and he explained it had taken him some time to get used to it all. They discussed [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]] and their intention to create an army - something neither of them wanted to see. They hoped to help stop this, and to prevent any more creations like themselves suffering, and wondered what they should do about the [[Spark of Asantia]]. [[@Ichoren]] revealed he had lingering concerns about [[@Jack]]'s motivations in this regard, and didn't fully trust him when it came to this gemstone (thankfully [[@Jack]] was fast asleep during this conversation).

[[@Splatts]] and [[@Zharek]] listened in, albeit [[@Zharek]] was slightly distracted whilst he learned a handy new Teleportation spell. He quickly put this into practice, disappearing some of [[@Jack]]'s clothing (and greatly offending [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]] in the process).

The next day, the party continued their journey, eventually arriving at the great wall. Using the map [[@Splatts]] had found in the [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]] base, they were able to easily find the secret way through. It was a concealed doorway that opened into a long, narrow tunnel that passed through the huge stone wall. As they walked through, [[@Jack]] noticed footprints of several sets of boots in the dusty ground, indicating several others had passed through at some point.

The tunnel opened out into a wonderous land - a land of beauty unlike anything they'd ever seen. Sprawled ahead of them were lush green forests with two enormous and majestic mountains that climbed up on either side. Strange birds flew overhead and the land thrummed with life. Then, as they took in the glorious sight, they caught a whisper on the wind - "You don't belong here."

Undeterred, the party head into [[Asantia's Blessing]]...

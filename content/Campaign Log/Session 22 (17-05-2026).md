*Luminar 113 (Afternoon), Nethirial*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Cove-Candle]]  

The party emerged from the portal, finding themselves in a dusty chamber lit by thin slits in the ceiling that funneled light from somewhere high above. The air was warm and stale. They checked themselves, reeling from the pain of the teleportation, and [[@Ichoren]] expressed no desire to do that again.

Ahead was a huge stone door. [[@Jack]] opened it to reveal a long corridor beyond, the floor littered with sand. Eventually, they came to a wall that had clearly been erected in haste some time ago, and wide cracks in the mortar revealed a corridor beyond. [[@Ichoren]] was able to shove his way through the stone with ease, crumbling the wall.

At the end of the following corridor, the party ascended steps and emerged into a huge temple, adorned with gold and great statues. They heard the voice of a priestess preaching to her congregation, reciting a prayer to [[Yllara]], the goddess of harvest.

They decided to wait and hid from view until the service was over. Then [[@Splatts]] went out to speak to the lone priestess. She was welcoming but clearly confused by his presence. When the rest of the party emerged she became visible scared, realising they were visitors from afar. She attempted to placate the party and leave them to get supplies, but [[@Zharek]] probed her mind learning she feared being accused of conspiring with spies of the Empire, and intended to seek help from the guards. [[@Zharek]] teleported over and held her tight. She kicked and tried to scream, but [[@Splatts]] was eventually able to calm her.

Realising they posed no direct threat, the priestess - **Troxy** - settled and they were able to question her. They confirmed they had made it to [[Nethirial]], but realised all might not be as simple as they'd hoped. The [[Emperor of Nethiria]] had not been seen in a very long time, known to be a recluse hiding away in the palace. The Hand of the Emperor, an the elven woman known as [[Xinia]], relayed his commands. The Emperor ruled with terror, crushing any who opposed his decrees. There was widespread famine, pestilence, and strife. 

Troxy offered to help the party and supplied them with robes used by the choir to help disguise their unusual appearance. At the party's request, she directed them to a nearby tavern where they could rest. 

When they left the temple, the party entered wide square surrounded by sandstone buildings. A great stone palace stood ahead, and directly behind that an imposing pyramid.

They saw a crowd had gathered in the square, clearly compelled by stern, ghoulish-looking guards clad in copper armour. The crowd surrounded a large wooden platform, at the center of which stood a terrible guillotine. After two guards led an aged man to the device, one stood forward and loudly decreed the man would face death for crimes against the Empire and for "taking what did not belong to him". Several cries of anguish from the crowd followed the great thud of the dropped blade.

As the crown solemnly disbursed, several noticed the party but hurried about their own business. The people here looked poor, weary, and downtrodden. There was general sense of unease all around.

A guard watched from the platform, noticing the party and pointing for another to go after them. The party scattered coins and rations causing the crown to swarm in their desperation to collect the items. [[@Ichoren]] and [[@Jack]] were able to vanish with the distraction, and the others fled before the guards could find them. They disappeared into the **Hairy Wolfkin** tavern, shedding their robes as they entered.

Inside [[@Splatts]] presented 10 gold pieces to the proprietor, seeking board and food. Shocked by the value of the gold before him, he took only two coins (covering expenses for them all for some considerable time), and offered to assist them in any way he could. 

They spoke in a quiet back room, and the party asked him about the Empire. He expressed his discontent with the Emperor's rule and his dislike of [[Xinia]]. The party learned magic was outlawed on penalty of death, and the rules of the Emperor were harsh. He did, however, tell them about a resistance that had gathered quietly in the shadows.

In the night [[@Splatts]] once again dreamed, hearing the kindly voice speak from the nothingness. The voice encouraged him to continue - he was so close now. She reminded him of all the party had achieved, against all odds. When [[@Splatts]] woke, [[The Emberglass]] was again glowed in his satchel but gradually faded. 

In the morning the party found their way to a nearby alley and a door bearing the symbol of the resistance - the "N.O." A halfling named **Basil** granted them entry, overjoyed at the prospect of new members joining, especially as all the previous members had been beheaded. [[@Jack]] was pleased the join, but the others though were less than convinced with Basil's competence. However, they learned he had worked (for a very short and unproductive time) as a cook's assistant in the palace. The head cook, **Henrietta**, was always keen for new staff (they had a very high turnover, mainly due to beheadings). He also told them there was a secret way into the place via the sewers, and that the Emperor lived somewhere in the centre of the palace up stairs that were always guarded. Basil echoed the party's concerned that [[Xinia]] was not being truthful about the Emperor, and that he might actually be dead or being held against his will. He'd also heard [[Xinia]] had been seen to disappear through walls.

The party decided they would try and pose and cooks and get inside the palace to try and find the Emperor. They headed to one of the guard tents seeking work, and [[@Zharek]] impressed them with his culinary skills. They were granted work immediately, and sent off to the palace to get started...

*Luminar 114 (Morning), Nethirial*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Cove-Candle]]

The party confirmed their plan: [[@Jack]] would try to persuade [[Xinia]] to join him in her chamber, where he would "accidentally" discover the entrance to her secret chamber. The others would lie I wait there, ready to ambush her as she entered. 

And so they set it in motion. [[@Jack]] went to find [[Xinia]], seeing she was deep in conversation with her commander. He delivered her some fresh water and put the jug in her chambers, allowing him to signal the others the coast was clear. They quickly snuck through and into the secret ritual chamber. 

With a bit more effort than he'd have liked, and a few mocking sniggers from the helmed Commander, [[@Jack]] managed to persuade [[Xinia]] to join him for a "private meeting".

He awaited her in her chamber as she sauntered in. She dispensed with the pleasantries - she wanted to get down to business. [[@Jack]] couldn't quite find the word to explain what he had in mind, so reached for the dictionary... and in doing so opened the door to the secret chamber. 

The others stood silent, trying to close their ears to [[@Jack]]'s flirting.

Seeing the door open, [[Xinia]] asked [[@Jack]] if he'd like to see her "special room". Of course [[@Jack]] did, so she led him in by the hand... but sensed something was not quite right.

But it was too late, as she stepped into the room her enemies pounced. 

[[Xinia]]'s eyes blackened as she summoned dark magic. "Fools!" she exclaimed.

[[@Zharek]] charged his Power Fist, ready to strike. 

[[Xinia]] uttered a dark incantation and a dark energy flowed from her amulet. She transformed her appearance into a being both demonic and terrifying. However, before the spell could take effect, [[@Zharek]] reacted and used his Magic Shield to interfere with her spell, much to her anger. She abandoned the attempt, and instead summoned a creature of the undead from the bones of the corpse in the room. 

This foul skeletal creature lunged forwards at [[@Zharek]], cutting into him with its rusty blade. 

[[@Ichoren]] swept [[Xinia]]'s legs and slammed her hard into the ground, knocking the wind from her lungs. 

[[@Splatts]] lunged for her amulet and missed, but [[@Jack]] threw himself upon her and managed to rip it free fromher neck, and quickly threw it across the room to [[@Cove-Candle]]. Both felt a sense of doom and dark energy as they held it, albeit briefly for [[@Jack]].

[[@Splatts]] seized the opportunity to strike at [[Xinia]] whilst she lay disadvantaged in the floor. He plunged his knife deep into her, causing her to scream in pain. Blood covered [[@Jack]].

As [[@Zharek]] dodged another strike from the skeleton and then smashed its head clean off, [[@Cove-Candle]] bumbled over to [[Xinia]]. Barely looking at what he was doing, and with a heartfelt apology in the process, [[@Cove-Candle]] buried his blade into her again, and again, and again... until she breathed her last breath and went still. They had slain the dark sorceress, and somehow come out relatively unscathed.

The party wasted no time taking some urgent next steps, unsure if the spell that held the guards had died with [[Xinia]].
- [[@Ichoren]] raced up the the prison chamber through the secret passage. He found the energy field had gone - the [[Emperor Nyin|Emperor]] was free! Both [[Emperor Nyin]] and [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]], overjoyed at their released, followed [[@Ichoren]] back down the way he came.
- [[@Zharek]] searched the ritual chamber, finding a few items of value and a spell book of Necromancy.
- [[@Splatts]] searched [[Xinia]], finding nothing but a small drawing of [[@Jack]] and an alluring pose. [[@Zharek]] happily relieved [[@Splatts]] of this burden and took it from him. 

[[Emperor Nyin]] was keep to reclaim his palace. However, the party asked him some important questions first, starting with [[Xinia]]'s amulet. He informed him it was fashioned out of a gemstone that ones belonged to twelve powerful stones. This amulet was named [[Salana's Curse]], named after the goddess [[Salana]] of death and destruction. It was a darkly powerful item and he warned them to take great care with it.

The party followed [[Emperor Nyin]] into the palace court. Initially stunned at his presence, the Commander and others in the chamber immediately bowed in reverence. All seemed freed of the spell that held their minds. [[Emperor Nyin]] told them he had returned, and [[Xinia]]'s services were no longer required.

The party spoke at length with [[Emperor Nyin]] and [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] about their next steps. They needed help with the war, and most importantly (to them) to save [[Nordvale]] and [[@Splatts]]' family. "We go to war!" exclaimed [[Emperor Nyin]], pleased to help them in any way he could. 

He instructed the Commander to assemble the army who in turn told him it would take him a day to gather the troops ready to march. It would take them a week or more to march to [[Nordvale]].

The party told him about the portals and suggested they use these. [[Emperor Nyin]] knew of these portals, and was surprised the party had used them as they were so unreliable (and dangerous) since [[The Rending]]. He said he would agree to send a small party through the portals, but not his whole army due to the risks involved.

[[@Zharek]] suggested the shortest route available - straight across the [[Weeping Deep]]. [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]] again would not risk his army in the mists, as terrible things were known to lurk there. However, if the party could prove the mists were safe or remove them entirely, they he would have his army sail across with all haste. He went on to explain the mists were unnatural, and something from the island was causing them. He also explained another of [[The Twelve]] may be on the [[Isle of Tharos]], as it was long ago given to a noble named **Grim Sunstar** who lived there.

Throughout their conversation [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|The Queen]] and [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]] told the party what they knew about the twelve gemstones that once made up the [[Dawnmother's Diadem]], each imbued with the spirit of one of her twelve daughters.

They told the party if all twelve gemstones were found the [[Dawnmother's Diadem]] could be remade. Should this fall into the wrong hands, then the darkest of times may fall upon [[Alethyria]].

Despite this, [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] presented [[@Splatts]] with her amulet - [[Selarune's Window]]. She expressed that, while she was unsure if it was wise to bring all the stones together, she could not overcome the feeling that the party were meant to have them. She could think of no others most suitable to hold them, and who would do what was right.

Afterwards, [[@Ichoren]] took a few moments to speak to [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] in private. He told her he was worried about being evil, having me made by [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]]. [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] made it quite clear that she judged him only be his deeds, and in her opinion he was quite the opposite - a creature who was pure of heart. They also spoke briefly about [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]] and how she was worried they might be working with the [[Um’brathir]], or intending to do so. [[@Ichoren]] promised he would help her defeat them when the time was right.

After [[Emperor Nyin|The Emperor]] took his leave to put himself to work running the palace and raising an army, the party collected their belongings and made their way to the portal chamber. They set the dial to the [[Isle of Tharos]], and stepped through...

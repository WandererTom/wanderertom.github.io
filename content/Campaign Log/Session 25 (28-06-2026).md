*Luminar 114 (Morning), Nethirial*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Cove-Candle]]

The party confirmed their plan: [[@Jack]] would try to persuade [[Xinia]] to join him in her chamber for another rendezvous. Then he would "accidentally" discover the entrance to her secret chamber, and hope that she lured him inside. The others would by waiting, ready to ambush her as she entered. 

Apprehensive but with time pressing upon them, they set the plan in motion.

[[@Jack]] found [[Xinia]] who was deep in conversation with her commander in the palace court. He delivered her some fresh water and told her he would place the jug in her chambers. Whilst inside,  he quickly signalled to the others the coast was clear. The rest of the party quickly snuck through her chance and through the other the secret passage into the ritual room, where they took up their positions. 

With a bit more effort than he'd have liked, and suffering a few mocking sniggers from the helmed Commander, [[@Jack]] managed to persuade [[Xinia]] to join him in her chambers for a "private meeting".

He led the way and she sauntered in behind him.  Insude, she dispensed with the pleasantries of the court - she wanted to get down to business. [[@Jack]] couldn't quite find the word to explain what he had in mind, so reached for the bookshelf and took hold of the dictionary... and in doing so the door to the secret chamber sprung open. [[Xinia]] pointedly did not react.

On the other side of the door the others waited in silence, trying to close their ears to the uncomfortable sounds of [[@Jack]]'s flirting.

Eventually, [[Xinia]] asked [[@Jack]] if he'd like to see her "special room" and led him in by the hand. As they entered she sensed something wasn't quite right.

But it was too late, as rounded the corner of the chamber her waiting enemies pounced. 

[[Xinia]]'s eyes immediately blackened as she summoned dark magic. "Fools!" she exclaimed. Combat erupted.

[[@Zharek]] charged his Power Fist, ready to strike. 

[[Xinia]] uttered a dark incantation and black energy flowed from her amulet. Her appearance transformed into something demonic and terrifying. However, before the spell could fully take effect, [[@Zharek]] reacted and used his Magic Shield to interfere with it. The black energy dissipated, much to her anger. She pivoted tactics, and with more dark speech summoned the undead from the bones of the corpse in the room. 

This foul skeletal creature lunged forward to [[@Zharek]] and cut into him with its rusty blade. 

[[@Ichoren]] swept [[Xinia]]'s legs and slammed her hard into the ground, knocking the wind from her lungs. 

As she fell, [[@Splatts]] lunged for her amulet but missed. Seeing what [[@Splatts]] had tried, [[@Jack]] threw himself upon [[Xinia]] and managed to rip the amulet free from her neck. He quickly threw it across the room to [[@Cove-Candle]]. Both felt a sense of doom as they held it, albeit the moment was brief for [[@Jack]].

While [[Xinia]] was prone on the ground, [[@Splatts]] seized the opportunity to strike hard and fast. He plunged his knife deep down into her. She screamed in pain as her blood covered [[@Jack]].

As [[@Zharek]] dodged another strike from the skeleton and then smashed its head clean off, [[@Cove-Candle]] bumbled his way over to [[Xinia]]. Barely wanting to look at what he was doing, and giving a heartfelt apology in the process, [[@Cove-Candle]] thrust his blade into her once, twice  and then again, and again, and again. The dark sorceress exhaled her last breath and fell still. To the party’s relief, they had slain her and somehow come out relatively unscathed.

They wasted no time taking some urgent next steps, unsure if the spell that controlled the guards had died with [[Xinia]].
- [[@Ichoren]] raced up the the prison chamber through the secret passage. He found the energy field had gone - the [[Emperor Nyin|Emperor]] was free! Both [[Emperor Nyin]] and [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]], overjoyed at their released, followed him back down the way he came.
- [[@Zharek]] searched the ritual chamber, finding a few items of value and a spell book of Necromancy.
- [[@Splatts]] searched [[Xinia]], finding nothing but a small drawing of [[@Jack]] in an alluring pose. [[@Zharek]] happily took this from [[@Splatts]] to relieve him of the burden. 

[[Emperor Nyin]] was keen to reclaim his palace. First, the party asked him some important questions, starting with [[Xinia]]'s amulet. He informed them it was fashioned from of a gem that once belonged to a collection of twelve powerful stones. This amulet was named [[Salana's Curse]], after the goddess of death and destruction. It was a darkly powerful item and he warned them to take great care with it.

The party followed [[Emperor Nyin]] into the palace court. Although they were initially stunned by his presence, the Commander and guards immediately bowed in reverence. They were freed of the spell that held their minds. [[Emperor Nyin]] told them he had returned, and [[Xinia]]'s services were no longer required.

The party spoke at length with [[Emperor Nyin]] and [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] about their next steps. They needed help with the war, and most importantly (to them) wanted to save [[Nordvale]] and [[@Splatts]]' family. 

[[Emperor of Nethiria|Emperor Nyin]] owed the party a great debt and offered his assistance in any way.  "We go to war!" he exclaimed.

He instructed the Commander to assemble the army. The Commander told him it would take him a day to gather the troops ready to march. It would take a week or more to march to [[Nordvale]].

Realising this would take too long, the party told [[Emperor of Nethiria|Emperor Nyin]] about the portals and suggested they use these. [[Emperor Nyin]] knew of these portals, and was surprised the party had used them as they were so unreliable and dangerous since [[The Rending]]. He would agree to send a small party through the portals, but would not risk his whole army.

[[@Zharek]] suggested an alternative, taking the shortest route available - sailing straight across the [[Weeping Deep]]. [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]] would not risk his army in the mists either - terrible things lurked there. However, if the party could prove the mists were safe or remove them entirely, then he offered to sail his army across the watery expanse with all haste. He went on to explain the mists were unnatural, and something from the [[Isle of Tharos]] might be causing them. He also explained another of [[The Twelve]] could be on the island as it was given long ago to a noble named **Grim Sunstar** who lived there.

Throughout their conversation [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|The Queen]] and [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]] told the party what they knew about the twelve gemstones that once made up the [[Dawnmother's Diadem]], each imbued with the spirit of one of her daughters. [[Emperor of Nethiria|Emperor Nyin]], much to his shame, lived during [[The Rending]] and it was his mage who caused it.

They told the party if all twelve gemstones were found the [[Dawnmother's Diadem]] could be remade. Should this fall into the wrong hands, then the darkest of times would fall upon [[Alethyria]].

Despite this, [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] presented [[@Splatts]] with her amulet - [[Selarune's Window]]. She expressed that, while she was unsure if it was wise to bring all the stones together, she could not overcome the feeling that the party were meant to have them. She could think of no others who would be better caretakers for them, and she knew they would do what was right with them.

Afterwards, [[@Ichoren]] took a few moments to speak to [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] in private. He told her he was worried about being evil, having been created by [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]]. [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] made it quite clear that she judged him only be his deeds, and in her opinion he was quite the opposite of evil - a creature who was pure of heart. They also spoke briefly about [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]] and how she was worried they might be working with the [[Um’brathir]], or intending to do so. [[@Ichoren]] promised he would help her defeat them when the time was right.

After [[Emperor Nyin|The Emperor]] took his leave and put himself to work running the palace and raising an army, the party collected their belongings and made their way to the portal chamber. They set the dial to the [[Isle of Tharos]], and stepped through...

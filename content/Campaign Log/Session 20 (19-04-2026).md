*Luminar 109 (Morning), Asantia's Blessing*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]]  

The [[Splatt Pack]] continued their journey, heading towards the centre of [[Asantia's Blessing]] and the area between the two huge mountain peaks. They came to a large river and a rlarge, thundering waterfall. They decided to try and cross using the various rocks present along it rather than continue along the road that seemingly would take them further away from their goal.

Unfortunately for [[@Ichoren]], who was first to run and jump onto the rocks, it didn't go so well. He failed, catastrophically, immediately slipping and falling into the water and being swept off the high waterfall. Thankfully, he just managed to cling to a protruding rock part way down, and was able to hoist himself back up by throwing his grappling hook and rope from his sack. [[@Zharek]] used his new spell to teleport the others across, despite the potential toll this would take on his willpower. Fortunately for him, he had quickly gotten to grips with the new spell and once again proved himself the greatest mage that ever lived, casting it with particular ease and efficiency.

Now over the river, the party noticed an ancient temple visible above the distant tree line. They decided to head there to explore and rest, taking a few hours to make their way through the surrounding forest. 

When they reached the temple, they found the entrance took them down underground into the darkness. Using torchlight they explored the dark stone corridors, until they reached several statues of people that [[@Jack]] realised were quite unnatural. He recalled tales of a creature - a "Stonemaker" (he couldn't remember the real name) - that turned people to such objects. The party turned on their heels and fled as quickly as they'd entered.

After a night's rest nearby, a spot of hunting, spell-learning, and (bad) flute practice, they retraced their route and picked up the road again.

Before too long they were greeted by a large, hooved minotaur - **Frederick Minatori** (or Mini to his friends). He questioned whether they should be on the road, pointing ahead to a crudely made sign that read, "The way ahead is barred to all." [[@Jack]] made it clear he though Mini had made that sign himself, and was able to convince Mini that it was his dying wish to take that road and see the sights beyond. Somehow this worked, and Mini did not obstruct the party any further, leaving them to continue.

After a few hours more walking, the party came into view of [[Edenfall]]. Huge rocks lay scattered across the land, remnants of the once great mountain that had been torn asunder by the magical catastrophe in [[The Rending]]. High above the rocky ruin menacing storm clouds gathered, casting lightning down to strike the rocks below.

Continuing towards their destination, the party stopped in their tracks when they saw a legion of ghostly soldiers crossing the road ahead. Each were grim-faced and appeared to be heading to war. They realised this was an echo of the great war from centuries past.

[[@Ichoren]] tried to hardness the energy inside him and tap into whatever it was that fueled these apparitions. It did not go well. One of the soldiers turned sharply to look at him, and [[@Ichoren]] soon found himself surrounded by menacing figures pointing ghostly spears. [[@Ichoren]] quickly released the energy he'd tapped into and they faded away. In parting, a soldier warned him, "Be wise where you step."

After resting again for the night, the  party began their ascent up the ruin of [[Edenfall]]. [[@Zharek]] attempted to scout the way ahead from the skies, but was buffeted down to the ground by the high winds, but thankfully caught by [[@Jack]] and [[@Splatts]].

They faced an arduous walk, and a rockslide soon caught them off guard. Most dived out of the way, but [[@Splatts]] stumbled, and seeing he was in trouble, [[@Ichoren]] dived to take the impact of the rocks and protect his friend. Fortunately, he suffered relatively little damage in the process. 

After cresting the low peak, they faced down into a wide basin that once was the mountain itself. Far at the bottom, between the broken crags, they saw a large, stone doorway and realised this was their destination.

Upon arriving, they found the doors shut. [[@Jack]] examined an inscription and in a flash of inspiration determined the meaning of the old language - that only the worthy may enter with a touch.

Despite trying, [[@Jack]] was unable to open the door, his thoughts focused on the [[Spark of Asantia]] and potential riches. [[@Splatts]] on the other hand, was far more humble and questioned whether he was even worthy to try to open the door. He ultimately sought only to help those in need. For him, the doors scraped open, revealing the way into the **Temple of Edenfall**...

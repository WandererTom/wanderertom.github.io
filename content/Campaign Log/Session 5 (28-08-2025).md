*Luminar 101 (Morning), Valesview*  
[[@Jack]], [[@Ichoren]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Zharek]]

As the party made their way towards Finch's Fings, [[Lord Ronald Squinketon|Lord Squinketon]] hastily caught up to them on his steed. Eager to speak to [[@Ichoren]], he awkwardly explained he might have been a tad hasty naming him a Duke but instead offered a lesser rank of nobility that was within his means to bestow. [[@Ichoren]] decided Count was the most befitting title for him.

[[Lord Ronald Squinketon|Lord Squinketon]] went on to ask [[@Ichoren]] to keep an ear out for information about the death of [[Tobias Squinketon]], as he yearned to know the truth about what happened.

He also passed a sealed note to be handed to [[@Cove-Candle]] when he reappeared. This honoured his promise of a boat. He explained the note should be delivered to **Oona Gentlebreeze** in the Shipwright's Quay of [[Morenthia]], and will authorise her to either build or provide an existing vessel at his expense (within reason).

On the door of Finch's Fings the party found a hastily scrawled note that read, "*Closed, I am relocating due to recent events. If you need everyday essentials, try Jeremy Saddlebag's shop next door.*" Next door the party found a small shed, barely standing and hardly noticeable from the road. It had a wobbly, crudely written sign over the front door labelled "[[Jeremy's Emporium of the Mundane]]." It was occupied by [[Jeremy]], and old human male with long straggly white hair who was overjoyed to greet his first customers of the Triad. He said Finch's Fings closing up was the best thing to happen as no-one had even realised his shop was there until then.

- [[@Jack]] tried to offload his stolen amulets without success (Jeremy had a slight cashflow problem). 
- [[@Ichoren]] bought a mundane sack (a stick with simple hessian sack on the end), although [[@Zharek]] later convinced him it was magical.
- [[@Splatts]] stamped on [[@Jack]]'s toe in annoyance, with surprising effect.
- [[@Splatts]] bought a unnamed brownish looking and very mundane bottle of perfume that smelled of an overcast day.
- [[@Zharek]] used Detect Magic to find Jeremy was hiding a single, non-mundane item; a small chest containing a dagger that was infused with an enchantment of Elementalism. Although [[@Zharek]] did not have enough gold to buy this directly, [[Jeremy]] said he was due a shipment from [[Morenthia]] that hadn't arrived, and agreed to an exchange of the dagger if [[@Zharek]] could find and return the goods.

As the party left [[Valesview]] on their way to [[Morenthia]], they crested the side of the valley and the view before them opened out into a wonderous but disturbing sight. Before them lay a valley that was until recently picturesque and serene, but now bore terrible scars of the explosion that had coursed out from [[Vadoria City]]. Proud and majestic trees that lined the valley lay scattered and broken; dominoes flattened from the singular direction. In the hazy distance at the head of the valley they could make out where the [[Evervein]] met [[Lake Vadoria]], and beyond this the crumbling towers of the city shimmered like a mirage in the day's warmth. Ominous clouds loomed over the capital, and swirled around in a slow, melancholic dance. The sky was unnaturally dim for the time of year. Down river, deeper into the valley nearer to them and cast in perpetually moving shadows of clouds, was the great walled city of [[Morenthia]]. The occasional column of smoke rose from the city, like thin black marks on the otherwise colourful sky. They followed the well trodden road that led down the valley towards [[Morenthia]], a distance that would likely take them two shifts to arrive.

[[@Ichoren]] was saddened by the destruction of the trees and took a piece of a large fallen Oak to preserve it.

As they walked, the party discussed recent events and questioned the intention of the red-skinned invaders. They thought the [[Queensguard]] and [[Valeguard]] would likely be overwhelmed. [[@Splatts]] took a positive attitude that they could do something to help, given how the events had intertwined them.

After walking for about three hours, the party came across a horseless cart stopped at the side of the road. Its wheel was broken, and around it lay several bodies of humans with deep blade wounds. [[@Zharek]] used Farsight to examine the scene before they approached, and found the cart contained various goods including those likely belonging to Jeremy. [[@Jack]] noticed two things with his keen senses; slight movement from within the cart, and the sound of a horse in the bushes nearby.

Inside the cart, the party found the sole survivor of the attack, the young [[Tibbin (Tibs) Figleaf]]. He said the attackers were red-skinned and his master whispered to him they were looking for something. They smashed the crates and searched the cart, discarding all the goods without taking anything. They found his master's black cloth sack and were momentarily excited before they realised it contained only some polished rocks he was selling, and so they killed him.

[[@Ichoren]] and [[@Splatts]] befriended [[Tibbin (Tibs) Figleaf|Tibs]]. [[@Ichoren]] started making a carving for him of his favourite animal, and [[@Splatts]] wondered if the attackers had been looking for the items on the [[InvaderScroll.webp|scroll]], but [[Tibbin (Tibs) Figleaf|Tibs]] didn't recognise them. Meanwhile, [[@Jack]] euthanised a poor arrow-wounded horse and found some gold in its saddlebags, and [[@Zharek]] flew back to Jeremy to report the location of his goods. After some bargaining, [[@Zharek]] persuaded Jeremy to part with the fancy enchanted dagger in exchange for the location of his goods despite them not having been returned. [[@Zharek]] found the dagger's blade was made of some kind of bone and it had an ornate wooden handle.

When the party reunited, they continued towards [[Morenthia]] but found two dead guards a short distance along the road. These had been shot with arrows of the same type [[@Jack]] discovered in the wounded the horse; finely crafted all black arrows with vicious serrated heads.

Moving more cautiously (but not as quietly as they hoped), they came to a fork in the road and encountered the well-dressed [[Lady Elira of House Vaelin]] who was alone, sat prim and proper on a suitcase. She demanded the party carry her bags and escort her to [[content/Places/Kingdom of Vadoria/Nordvale|Nordvale]], as her guards had run off a short while before for some disturbance and not returned. The party realised these were the guards they'd found dead on the road. [[@Ichoren]] walked off, offended at the lady's rudeness, and [[@Zharek]] ignored her. [[@Splatts]] expressed his dislike of her attitude. Only [[@Jack]] found the lady's favour, largely because he kissed her delicate gloved hand (that was adorned with a large sapphire ring), charmed her with complements about her "beautiful face adorning \[her] head", and a forced sickly polite manner much to [[@Splatts]]'s disgust. He even managed to sell to her several of his stolen items (she got a good price), and agreed to escort her to [[Morenthia]] and then by boat to [[content/Places/Kingdom of Vadoria/Nordvale|Nordvale]]. Although the lady was charmed, he still found himself carrying her bags. 

Now accompanied by both [[Tibbin (Tibs) Figleaf|Tibs]] and [[Lady Elira of House Vaelin|Lady Elira]], the party continued to [[Morenthia]]. Eventually, they neared the city and from their raised vantage point on the side of the vale they took in the view ahead of them. They they saw how the city was surrounded by large stone walls and sprawled out across the [[Evervein]]. The great river divided the nearest western quarter from the other districts of the city. The tall masts of numerous ships marked the location of docks in the north-west, and beyond this the wrecks of several vessels listed in the river, likely damaged in the explosion. The city's northernmost walls were partially damaged but intact, undergoing repairs. The westernmost gate - that nearest to the party - was open, and a throng of refugees flowed into the city. Guards were posted all around, tense and nervous. These guards did not wear the distinctive green capes of the [[Valeguard]] or the golden armour of the [[Queensguard]], but had upon them a variety of makeshift clothing and armour. The only [[Valeguard]] they saw was a small group that rode out north from the gate towards [[Vadoria City]], and in doing so passed a straggle of soldiers that stumbled back visibly injured and beaten.

Eager to find [[@Cove-Candle]]'s boat and continue their journey, the party made their way down towards the western gate of [[Morenthia]]...

*Luminar 114 (Morning), Nethirial*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Cove-Candle]]

In the dead of night, [[Xinia]] slept deeply upon the silk-covered bed, exhausted from the "service" of her newly appointed assistant. Awake beside her, [[@Jack]] took in his surroundings. Notably, he paid close attention to an amulet she had removed and placed upon her bedside table earlier on. He snuck over and picked it up. It contained a large, multifaceted gemstone, black in colour and far darker than anything he'd ever seen in his lifetime, despite his vast experience with gems. It seemingly absorbed light. It was set into an ornate amulet of pure silver, almost triangular in shape and with pointed features. The amulet felt strangely light and heavy at the same time. [[@Jack]] replaced it on the table, resisting the urge to steal it.

Meanwhile, the others rested and explored the palace a little further in the quiet, but found little of real note.

In the morning, [[@Zharek]] made his way over the the head guard and took up his offer to enlist in their ranks. He was put to work immediately and tasked with patrolling the palace. At some point he spoke with another guard, learning [[Xinia]] had taken other lovers before, and some had not been seen again.

The others made their way to the kitchen to commence their duty, except [[@Jack]] who returned to court to assist [[Xinia]]. He found her engaged in deep in conversation with the huge, armoured commander he'd seen the day before. With a twinkle in her eye, she instructed [[@Jack]] to bring her breakfast.

Convening near the kitchen, the party quietly planned. [[@Ichoren]] set off to explore the secret passageway and look for the other one behind the bookcase. The others planned to keep watch and distract where required.

Finding himself once against desired in [[Xinia]]'s chambers, [[@Jack]] reacted quickly and managed to persuade [[Xinia]] he should go on ahead and prepare the room for her arrival, buying [[@Ichoren]] some much needed time. When [[Xinia]] followed, she found [[@Jack]] stood confidently behind her desk, boldly revealing all. Unfortunately for [[@Jack]], his ill-considered dance managed to change her mood entirely and she berated him for treating her like a common harlot as she stormed out of the chamber. 

Fortunately, [[@Ichoren]] had already found his way through [[Xinia]]'s chambers and found the hidden door behind the bookcase. Beyond, he entered a dark antechamber arranged for some kind of ritual. At the centre was a partly skeletal corpse, surrounded by candles and strange markings. Around the room [[@Ichoren]] noted two large tomes, a statues of a skull and a strange shrine. He made his way through a second door, and then ascended a staircase that took him into a long corridor on the upper floor.

At the end of this corridor he came to another door, and opened it to find himself in what he realised was a special prison. Trapped behind a magical energy field was [[Emperor of Nethiria|Emperor Nyin]] himself. The [[Emperor of Nethiria]] was intrigued to see a new visitor, and they spoke briefly to discover they were aligned in purpose.

Wasting no time, [[@Ichoren]] passed [[The Emberglass]] through the energy field. The Emperor took it reverently, moved to the cntre of the chamber, and let go - the orb floated in the air. Then, he muttered words [[@Ichoren]] did not understand. [[The Emberglass]] disappeared in a blinding flash, and [[@Ichoren]] saw [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] stood before him in its place. She stretched, finally freed of the confines of [[The Emberglass|The Orb]].

[[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]] stood awkwardly as he realised he'd trapped her with him in the prison in his haste to release her.

With time of the essence, [[@Ichoren]] asked how he might free them. [[Emperor of Nethiria|Emperor Nyin]] explained there were two possible ways he could think of: either [[@Ichoren]] recovered the Emperor's staff so he could regain his power ([[Xinia]] had hidden this somewhere within the dark and dangerous **Crypt of Unan**), or they could kill [[Xinia]] and see if that released the magic holding him. [[@Ichoren]] learned that [[Xinia]] had some kind of hold over the guards and servants, but [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]] thought he might still have loyalists. He expressed that he'd been trapped there for a *very* long time.

Terrified [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] would be discovered in the prison, [[@Ichoren]] returned and reconvened with the party and told them what had happened. En route he nabbed one of the tomes from [[Xinia]]'s hidden chamber, and [[@Zharek]] read it to find it contained numerous incantations and ramblings about dark magic, with references to **Salana** and life beyond death. 

[[@Zharek]] heard footsteps outside, and they hushed. Unbeknown to them, Isaac delivered a meal to the guards.

Fortunately, [[@Zharek]] soon head out into the corridor, in time to see Isaac returning to the kitchen with an empty tray. He panicked, fearing the guards might soon discover [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]]. He dashed through the secret passageways as [[@Splatts]] struggled to keep up, and just managed to arrive in the Emperor's prison as the guard arrived through the other doors. The guard looked at him in confusion, but thankfully was focussed on [[@Zharek]] and not who was inside the cell. [[@Zharek]] tried to dominate the guard's mind and take control of him, but found another's magic had already taken grip of him. At that, he cast Dispel and caused the guard to suddenly appear confused. Realising the guard was no longer under [[Xinia]]'s control, [[@Zharek]] and [[@Splatts]] convinced him to help them. The guard agreed, and vowed to keep their presence secret whilst they planned their next steps. The party knew time was of the essence, for the guard would only be on shift for a few more hours.

[[@Splatts]] spoke with [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]], realising it was her voice that had guided him during his strange dreams. She explained it was the only way she could communicate. He asked how and why she had ended up in [[The Emberglass|The Orb]]. She told him it was a provision of escape setup long ago, if either ruler was ever in dire need. She had seen a vision of the impending attack granted to her by her amulet, and had managed to flee the city - secretly carried away by her guard servant - just in time. She now had much to discuss with [[Emperor of Nethiria|Emperor Nyin]].

Leaving the prison chamber in the hope that [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] would not be discovered, the party gathered once more. After much quiet discussion, they agreed a plan: The party would ambush [[Xinia]] in her chambers (if [[@Jack]] could lure her back in), take the dark amulet from her, and then end her wicked life...

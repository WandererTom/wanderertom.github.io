*Luminar 107 (Morning), Cabal Everwood Base*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Cove-Candle]]

Footsteps approached. [[@Ichoren]] looked over at the prisoners, concerned they would call for help. He dashed over, nearly stumbling in the process but was caught by [[@Jack]]. They both cautiously guarded the scientists, warning them not to make a sound.

[[@Zharek]] and [[@Cove-Candle]] listened careful to two approaching dwarves, grumbling under their breath about the impromptu visit from the Grand Master and how he was always checking up on them. Tbh lingered outside the door to the caverns, in quiet discussion. [[@Zharek]] used Telepathy to implant a thought of hunger in one of their minds, causing them to move on to the kitchen.

Deciding not to investigate the hideout further, the party escaped through the secret passageway [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]] had told them of, leading them up to the main entrance. They took the head scientist with them to prevent an immediate alarm, leaving the other two tied up and moved deep into the caverns. 

The party debated what to do with the scientist. He told them they would be hinted mercilessly by [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]] for what they had done. He offered to help smooth this over, to ingratiate them with the Grand Master, expressing that he would be pleased to see [[@Ichoren]]. However, [[@Ichoren]] was having none of it. 

[[@Jack]] decided the only way to ensure they were safer for a while was to remove the evidence - the head scientist. He took out his dagger, but realised he couldn't do it. [[@Ichoren]] took over, dragging the scientist out and - despite his efforts to negotiate - slit his through. He hid the body in the woodland. He returned to the others, having detached emotionally from what he'd done.

The party made their way hastily to their ship where they met [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]] and "Sistoren". [[@Ichoren]] named her [[Aetheren Clay]]. They set sail to [[Duskmire]], intending to hug the coast sound to [[Asantia's Blessing]].

As evening set in the party docked at [[Duskmire]], seeing several somber fisher-folk with poor catches. [[@Jack]] nodded in solidarity, given his own failed attempts to fish along the river. 

The old fishing town was dilapidated and near-abandoned, but they made their way in seeking provisions and rest. 

[[@Cove-Candle]] spoke to a local fisherman named **Lucas**, bartering with him in exchange for two haddocks. During this conversation he questioned by [[@Cove-Candle]] had such poor memory, saying he reminded him of a lad called **Martin** who had washed up on the shore not too long ago. He couldn't remember a thing about who he was or where he'd come from, but there wasn't a mark on him. It was very strange. Martin was staying at the nearby tavern.

The party head into the bustling **Drunken Dwarf**. [[@Zharek]] purchased some promised pickled onions for [[Jeremy]], and the others rested for the night (at [[@Jack]]'s generous expense).

During the evening [[@Cove-Candle]] blended with the fisher-folk, telling tales of salmon. As they drank and chatted, he heard a story that caught his attention from an old sailor - a tale of a Niad. Few have seen her, but stories tell that she lives on a mysterious island that appears out of nowhere. Some have gone looking for her, seeking a great gem she owns. Few have ever returned, most doomed to an unknown fate or to return as a shell of the person they were. He thought that was what happened to Martin.

The party rested for the night, intending to speak with **Martin** in the morning and then make their way over towards [[Ombrin's Beacon]].

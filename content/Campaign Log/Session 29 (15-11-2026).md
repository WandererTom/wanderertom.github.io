*Luminar 115 (Evening), Veilhold*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Cove-Candle]]  

High atop the northern cliffs of the [[Isle of Tharos]], the party looked out over the vast [[Weeping Deep]]. They saw sail after sail come into view - a huge armada venturing forth from the west. The party hurriedly stoked their beacon, causing flames to raise high into the sky in an effort to draw the attention of the distant ships. 

It worked. The vessels made their way towards the island. At the head of the fleet was a huge galleon, and as it approached the shore, the party saw both [[Emperor Nyin]] and [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] aboard. They quickly ran down to the shore and made their way aboard. Then after some greetings, and with [[@Cove-Candle]] at the helm, they made haste towards the east coast of the great inland sea, heading directly for the [[Evervein]] and then on to [[Nordvale]].

As they sailed the wide river, night fell and most of the crew rested. [[@Jack]] spent a few hours fishing as [[@Ichoren]] watched in quiet contemplation. He told [[@Jack]] of his worries about [[@Splatts]]'s family and whether they could save them. [[@Jack]] offered a few words of encouragement (which did not come naturally to him).

In the early hours, [[@Ichoren]] noticed [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|The Queen]] taking a quiet walk on the deck. He took the opportunity to ask her about the magic inside him. She told him she knew little more about it, but the fragment of the [[Spark of Asantia]] that powered him had been in her family for generations before it was stolen. She told him there was a mage who lived in the [[Tower of Arcantia]] named **Moon Quietfoot** who might know more. 

Having sailed through the night, the fleet finally arrived at [[Nordvale]]. It was a far cry from the bustling, market town they once knew. Fear of the invading army weighed heavily upon its residents. Anxious guards patrolled the walls.

Overjoyed to see them arrive, **Tone** rushed over. They quickly learned the enemy's **Envoy** had returned the previous day to make a final demand for [[The Emberglass]]. The Day of Long Shadows was quickly approaching, and the enemy was soon to attack. As the enemy gathered around them, the residents of [[Nordvale]] had done their best to prepare for the onslaught, although many had little hope of victory. Their spirits had been lifted when the [[Bladedancers]] arrived a few days earlier, and now the unexpected arrival of the [[Nethirian Empire|Netherian]] fleet buoyed them more still. But **Tone** still believed they we terribly outmatched, and they had only a couple of days to turn the tide. 

The party discussed a plan. [[@Jack]] suggested they create a replica of [[The Emberglass]] and try to pass this off to buy them some time and, hopefully, secure the release of [[@Splatts]]' family. Thankfully, **Tone** knew **Bill the Blower**, who was an above-average glass blower (and conveniently very close by), and so [[@Jack]] was able to talk him through the details of his hastily concocted plan. [[@Jack]] gave **Bill** a pair of fish eyes for luck, which he accepted with reverence from his hero.

**Tone** gave the party a tour of the town. As they climbed the eastern battlements, they could see a terrible view before them. A few miles out, camped in the foothills, lay a huge enemy war host. Tent after tent marked the seemingly endless bivouac. Huge columns of smoke raised up into the bright sky.

However, closer to the town, just outside its walls, was the camp of the [[Bladedancers]]. [[@Ichoren]] spotted a very disheveled looking **Mikey** who waved up at him enthusiastically.

Eventually the party head over to the town hall to discuss their plan. They decided they needed more help, and fast. They managed to secure carrier pigeons, and wrote three letters:

The first was to [[Lord Ronald Squinketon|Lord Squinketon]], penned by [[@Jack]]. It initially read something along the lines of, "*Dear Lordy, hope you are well, we have found the Queen, we are in Nordvale, it will be taken soon. Send knights, and more if you can. Here's a piece of gold, spend it wisely. Hugs and kisses, Jack.*"

[[@Splatts]] questioned weather it was wise to mention [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]], in case the message was intercepted. [[@Ichoren]] tore it up. After some challenges finding suitable words, and several more failed attempts by [[@Jack]] resulting in much wasted parchment, [[@Ichoren]] went to get [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|The Queen]] herself. To the great amazement of the gathered soldiers and townspeople, she accompanied him to the town hall and penned words in her own fluent script: "*Dearest Ronald, I once gave you a great gift, and now I ask for one in return. Come to Nordvale and bring with you all the nights to can muster. Make haste, a war host approaches*."

Following this, [[@Ichoren]] penned a letter to [[Uthimial]], reading, "*Uthimial, we may not have left on the best of terms, but I am not one who can stand by when I see injustices and pain and suffering. You may be able to prevent a lot of that. I have no doubt the Um’brathir will stop when they reach you and you will be next in line when the others have fallen. I am asking your help. We are in Nordvale and we don't have long. Signed, the Splatts Pack.*"

And finally, [[@Jack]] tried again, this time writing to the [[Beaky Blinders]]: "*Alright geez, how you all doin? We need some payback. We're in Nordvale with the bee. Send all the lads and their caps, by order of The Beaky Jacks*."

 [[@Jack]] added a gold coin to the letters except [[Uthimial]]'s.

As the pigeons took flight - a couple struggling as they were slightly weighed down - the party considered what they could do over the next few hours to make the most of the time they had.

[[@Zharek]] instructed several of the guards to set dynamite traps outside the walls using the explosives they had previously collected for him. He then decided to scout the [[Um’brathir]] camp. He flew up into the sky and used his Far Sight to assess it. After some searching, he came to a collection of large tents. Here he saw a young, female mallard with her hands in manacles get escorted from a guarded tent into what appeared to be a privy. After a short time, she was roughly escorted back.

Realising he had seen a captive - and probably one of [[@Splatts]] family - he dropped back down with the news. **Bill** arrived with a large, glass ball he had created - giving them some semblance of a fake [[The Emberglass|Emberglass]] only without any magical glowing. 

[[@Zharek]] asked if anyone knew of a little robin that often flew around, and soon found out that [[Jeremy]] was very well known in these parts - and he liked to stay in a nearby bell tower. [[@Zharek]] called him down, and despite being unable to offer any pickled onions, convinced his little feathered friend to do them a favour - deliver a message to the leader of the [[Um’brathir]] instructing him to meet them in the place between the two armies. Here they would try to make their exchange.

Afterwards, [[@Jack]] sought to find someone who could harness the magic of Illusionism to imbue their new but rather dull glass orb with a bit of energy, and give it the appearance of a magical item. He learned the **Great Ricardo Shortworth**, a travelling halfling magician, was in town - and some said he knew a bit more magic then he often let on. 

**Tone** also offered to use his blacksmith skills to upgrade some of their weapons while they waited.

And so while the party anxiously awaited the responses to their letters, they set out to resupply and track down the diminutive illusionist...

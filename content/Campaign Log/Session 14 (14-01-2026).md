*Luminar 104 (Morning), Alzar's Tower*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Cove-Candle]]

The doored to [[Alzar's Tower]] opened, revealing a wide circular entrance hall. Inside, a small overcoat hung from a peg on the wall near the entrance, above a pair of well worn leather boots. The floor was an intricate tiled mosaic, arranged into the symbol of the eye the party had seen on the outside of the tower. 

The tower ascended into what looked like the night sky—but inside. Bright stars peeked out from behind clouds hanging softly several hundred meters above. A spiral staircase corkscrewed up the inside of the tower, disappearing into the cloudy darkness above.

[[@Jack]] took the overcoat from the peg, finding it far too small to fit him (despite trying). He rummaged through the pockets, but was caught by [[@Ichoren]] and [[@Zharek]] and returned the coins he'd found after their disapproval.

[[@Zharek]] walked to the middle of the chamber, standing directly on the centre of the eye. He felt a strong magical energy around him—Mentalism. He felt he could do something with it, but wasn't quite sure what...

The others started the long walk up the spiral staircase. [[@Ichoren]] carried [[@Splatts]] on his shoulders, and [[@Zharek]] picked up [[@Cove-Candle]] like a mother cat holding her litter, and flapped his wings to rise them both into the air.

After a long and strenuous walk —for some of them—they passed through the clouds and arrived, exhausted, at a small doorway. [[@Ichoren]] knocked, and the door swung open.

Beyond the doorway was a huge, open cylindrical chamber—the top of the tower. High above the roof curved into a bell-like shape, and birds fluttered between the rafters. The light in the room was a polychromatic wonder; rays of all colours danced through the numerous stained glass windows above, blending with the flickering light of several sconces on the wall further down.

Around the entire circumference were bookshelves—shelf after shelf of old tomes that reached high up to the height of three normal ceilings. The room smelled of musty old books, and mixed with the fresh scent of herbs and some kind of incense. 

Around the room were several large, wooden tables, piled high with books, scrolls, and all manner of other clutter. Sat cross-legged on a wobbly pile of tomes, was small and very old frog kin, with bright green wrinkly skin, huge eyes magnified by even bigger spectacles, and a pointy hat that had obviously been patched many more times than it should've been. The old frog pondered over tattered old book, muttering to a little robin perched nearby. He seemed not to have noticed the party, or was choosing to ignore them.

[[@Splatts]] approached and saw he was reading a tome entitled *The Insightful Insights of Torfin the Magnificent*. He enquired about the book, but the frog—the great mage [[Uthimial]]—discarded it in the air and it crashed down somewhere behind him. [[@Splatts]] took out [[The Queen's Note]] and then [[The Emberglass]] and this got his attention...

With a magical flourish, [[Uthimial]] extinguished the lights and raised [[The Emberglass|The Orb]] into the air with magical powers he weilded with ease. As the room was bathed in its red glow, he enquired what the party knew of it. After some conversation, and realising they knew very little, he decided to enlighten them. He floated down from the pile of times, and as the party sat, fascinated, he enlightened them with a story of old. As he spoke he formed floating images of the land of [[Alethyria]] and the party watched the events he spoke of...

> [!quote] Uthimal's Story
> 
> To understand the meaning of this Orb, you must first understand the Pact that forged a bond between nations when none thought it possible.
> 
> Our story begins 750 Triads past, when the goddesses walked freely with all kin and the lands of [[Alethyria]] were ever so different. For in those times the [[Kingdom of Vadoria]] and the [[Nethirian Empire]] existed in unity. That was until, of course, they came to discover the land of [[Asantia's Blessing]]. Such was the beauty and wonder of this land, both nations coveted it deeply for themselves. And so, war came to them, and friendship was cast aside.
> 
> The war took its toll, but neither nation relented. That was until [[The Great Mage of Nethiria]] discovered something secret deep within [[Asantia's Blessing]], something hidden, something immensely powerful. Under the direction of [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]], the mage sought to harness this power and gain supremacy over their foe. But it was not to be, for whether naive, corrupted, or simply overwhelmed, the mage released a deadly magic that brought calamity upon all the land. It was known as The Rending, and it was was ruin.
> 
> From the ashes that remained, a pact was forged, an binding agreement between rulers to never seek out such malign power again, and to leave [[Asantia's Blessing]], the [[Disputed Lands]], once and for all.
> 
> That much, I am sure, is at least vaguely familiar to you all. But what few know is that within the Pact secret agreements were made, secrets known only to a few, and never to be recorded in the annals of time. For [[King Thandor]] of [[Kingdom of Vadoria|Vadoria]] and [[Emperor of Nethiria|Emperor Nyin]] of [[Nethiria]]—their differences set aside—forged another agreement, a promise of aid should ever they face a dire calamity, in whatever form that might take. And with this Pact came the protocol, that the rulers themselves would send signal, a message contained in one of only two objects and such that could never be fabricated nor forged \[he showed two orbs being exchanged by the rulers\].
> 
> And so the Pact was made, and such it has existed hence. In the time that followed, the wise rulers rebuilt their lands and closed off [[Asantia's Blessing]] to shut it away. The goddesses, shamed by their subjects, withdrew back into the heavens. Centuries passed, and what was known as fact became memory, and then what was memory became myth.
> 
> Something we do know, however, is that during the early triads of this New Age, the nations of [[Kingdom of Vadoria|Vadoria]] and [[Nethiria]] became increasingly isolated and distant from one another. The wisest of their scholars and magic users saw the folly of this and so formed a secret collective. Through their bond, they dedicated themselves to watch over their lands and remain attuned and vigilant to the signs of malevolence and dark magics. So it was the [[Order of the Watchful Mages]] came to be, founded by the great mage Alzar in this very tower. Those who join the Order swear an oath of impartiality; to observe and advise but never to intervene, a means safeguard against the abuse of great magical power that must be so carefully wielded.
> 
> As for the true cause of [[The Rending]], these secrets were fragmented and hidden, the truth buried deep in the lands from whence it came. Rumours swirled for a time that whatever it was [[The Great Mage of Nethiria]] discovered was fragmented and remade into other forms, but even these closely guarded secrets remain unknown to the [[Order of the Watchful Mages|The Order]].
> 
> However, there are some who likely know even what the great mages do not. [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]] perhaps still lives, and we are sure the human rulers of [[Kingdom of Vadoria|Vadoria]] pass the secrets of their lineage from generation to generation.
> 
> For the elven [[Emperor Nyin|Emporer]], well, none truly know whether he still lives, and if he does, whether his mind has the clarity to recall the events of many centuries past. No Vadorian has entered his lands for many triads, and [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]] hides himself from even his own great mages, sending the occasional degree by letter alone.
> 
> And so much time has passed by, with the [[Emperor Nyin|Emporer]] shut away and the [[Kingdom of Vadoria]] existing in solitary ignorance. That was until today, when through the doors of my own tower came an unusual group of fellows carrying the very [[The Emberglass|Emberglass]] owned by [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|The Queen]] herself...

The party were in awe of [[Uthimial]]'s performance and skill with magic. When his story had concluded, they asked questions and he told them the following additional information:
## The Task

This was simple in nature but not in execution. The party must take [[The Emberglass|The Orb]] to [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]], if he lives, and if not, to whomever rules in his stead. This task carried the weight of the Kingdom, and [[Emperor Nyin|The Emporer]] would be duty bound to act and come to the aid of [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|The Queen]] and her Kingdom.

He described three possible routes for their journey to the capital city of [[content/Places/Nethirian Empire/Nethirial]]:

- **NORTH** - Through the [[Jagged Peaks]] via the Ambrine Pass. These are dangerous mountain passes and tunnels, inhabited by all manner of foul and evil beings. There are tales of demons and dragons in these parts, although many believe this to be myth. The Dunren inhabit the mountains, and are notoriously unwelcoming to guests. Goblins bands and other creatures roam and raid from the mountains.

- **EAST** - Across the Weeping Deep, but none have successfully sailed across in hundreds of triads. Only a handful who have attempted have ever returned, and they'd either lost their minds or their memories. He suggested if the party favoured this route they should seek advice from fisherfolk in [[Duskmire]] as this small fishing town contains some residents who are experienced on the water, albeit they don't venture too far from the shore.

- **SOUTH** - This route goes through [[Asantia's Blessing]]. Entry through the great wall is sealed and only [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn]] can grant passage (using a magical ward). However, he heard whispers the [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]] have found a way to gain access, but he does not know how.
## The Cabal

He said, "Not all mages exercise the caution of the Order. Some, unfortunately, believe those with the ability to wield magics should have greater power - and that they are the rightful rulers of these lands." 

He explained [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone]] are very much a real organisation led by mages with dark desires. Their true motivations aren't even known to [[Order of the Watchful Mages|The Order]], but he knows they desperately seek to reclaim lost, powerful magics. They maneuver in the shadows, for now, and he fears to what end.
## Caldurax

[[@Zharek]] showed him the painting he'd found in [[City Hall]] of the Karkion Mage. [[Uthimial]] knew of [[Caldurax]], but did not recognise the name "Kiri Nug" on the painting.

He said he knew of a Karkion historian named **Ysaria**, who lived in a place known as the **Hollow Archive** somewhere in the [[Everwood]]. She would likely know far more than he.

He did, however, describe [[Caldurax]] as a once majestical land with floating cities, home to the Karkion. It existed in what is now known as the [[Wyrmbane Expanse]]. The Calduraxian people never involved themselves in the war, yet were terrible victims of it.

Their kingdom fell soon after [[The Rending]], when the magic infusing the land was shattered by the cataclysmic event. Cities fell from the skies and crashed down. Many of their people perished. Those who escaped fled north to the [[Nethirian Empire|Empire]] or west to [[Kingdom of Vadoria|The Kingdom]] where they settled. 

[[Uthimial]] had great admiration and sympathy for the Calduraxian people, because of their love for magic and because they were unintended and innocent victims of the war. History tells them to be a kind people with a great love of the magics that resonate in all things. 

He recognised [[@Zharek]]'s medallion as a symbol of the [[Caldurax]] Royal Mages, although their name escaped him. These mages were supremely powerful but only used their power to benefit others. He said [[@Zharek]] should take great care of his medallion.
## Spark of Asantia

After some reluctance to ask about it (and a brief telepathic conversation with [[Uthimial]]), [[@Jack]] was encouraged by the others to not hold secrets. 

[[Uthimial]] knew the [[Spark of Asantia]] was a fabled gem infused with the spirit of [[Asantia]] herself. It has the power of life and beauty. While many believe it to be myth, he confirmed the gem exists and is a closely guarded secret. 

The spark takes the form of an egg shaped gemstone, and is said to glow with a perpetual radiance.

[[Order of the Watchful Mages|The Order]] believed it was closely linked to [[The Rending]] and heard it was damaged and fragmented. This suspicion increased because they heard the Aurelwyn royal household had a small fragment of it. However, the fragment was stolen from the palace several months ago, they believe by a spy working at the palace and suspect [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]] were responsible. The Queen was unusually enraged and entrusted a loyal member of her personal guard, [[Tobias Squinketon]], to investigate, but he was killed in the process. 

As for the gem-major, he could not say where this was or who, if anyone, had it. Even the records of [[Order of the Watchful Mages|The Order]] are vague, but suggest it was hidden away somewhere in [[Asantia's Blessing]], feared for the great power it holds.
## The Um'brathir

He said, "Rumours have persisted of an alliance of nations that seeks what does not belong to them, but none have dared venture across the seas to seek the truth of these matters. Sadly, it appears the truth has come to us."

---

[[@Ichoren]] was angry that [[Uthimial]] could not help them more—that [[Order of the Watchful Mages|The Order]] would only watch but not take action. [[Uthimial]] tried to calm him, placing a hand on [[@Ichoren]]'s chest and telling him he knew his heart was special. He said [[@Ichoren]]'s friends would need him, whatever path they chose to take.

[[@Splatts]] was distraught to realise [[Uthimial]] would not provide the help he'd hoped for to save his family. As he talked, he realised that perhaps they could achieve the task and—if they were lucky—return in time, or find another way to do both. He recognised the importance of the task, but the cost to him was too high.

[[@Ichoren]] couldn't bare the thought of abandoning [[@Splatts]] family, and the party discussed options.

Inspired, [[@Zharek]] engaged the help of [[Jonathan]], the little red-breasted robin, and made a deal—[[Jonathan]] would fly off and recruit as much help to protect [[Nordvale]] as possible, and in reward he promised to find him all the pickled onions he could eat. [[Jonathan]] agreed, and after taking directions to various large towns and the possible location of the [[Bladedancers]], he rallied his little bird friends and they took to the skies.

In respect to the party's next moves, [[@Jack]] was very keen to head through [[Asantia's Blessing]]...

[[@Cove-Candle]] was unsure what to do, expressing he was mainly present as an emotional support duck. When discussing family, he went on to say he thought he had a mother but he didn't remember what she looks like—or remember a lot for that matter.

Eventually, [[@Ichoren]] made a stunning confession. He told the group that used to be part of [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]], and so he thought he might be able to get them into [[Asantia's Blessing]]. [[@Jack]] was taken aback, wondering why so many had trust issues with him after that revelation...

The party decided to head to the [[Everwood]] to find [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]], and make their plan from there in the hope they could find a away into [[Asantia's Blessing]], and perhaps, save [[@Splatts]] family too.

At the conversations came to a close, [[Uthimial]] took the party into an adjoining room filled with rare items; some magical, some extremely useful. As promised by [[The Queen's Note]], he allowed each of the party to choose one of these to aid them in their quest. Of course, [[@Jack]] had to eat first.

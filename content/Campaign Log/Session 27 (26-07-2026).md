*Luminar 115 (Morning), Veilhold*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]]

[[@Jack]] assessed his surroundings. He was in a dark chamber, his hands bound with rope and mouth gagged, and [[@Zharek]] lay unconscious beside him. Around him in the gloom he could make out cages and many bones.

For a moment he thought he was in luck - his lockpicks were still hidden within his clothing. Only he realised they would be useless to escape from his rope bindings. However, he managed to move himself around the room, and came across a rusty sword that freed him from his bonds. 

Using the soggy sock that had gagged his mouth, he woke [[@Zharek]]. As both collected themselves, they heard footsteps approaching. They rushes either side of the doorway, and waited to ambush whoever came in.

**Marcus**, one of the congregants from the previous night's meal, entered. They quickly set upon him, seizing their chance to escape. [[@Zharek]] struck him, and [[@Jack]] attempted to grapple him but Marcus was too fast, and turned the tables taking hold of [[@Jack]] in a choke hold from the rear. He wheeled around, buying himself space and using [[@Jack]] as a shield. However, [[@Jack]] soon managed to free himself, causing Marcus to pull out his dagger and take a defensive position, one eye on the doorway. [[@Ichoren]] rushed him and managed to disarm him from the knife, causing it to clatter to the ground. 

When the moment arose, Marcus rushed for the doorway. [[@Zharek]] managed to land a blow and [[@Jack]] slashed at him causing a wound to his back. As Marcus fled down the corridor, [[@Zharek]] plucked a skull from the ground and launched it towards him, knocking him clean out and causing him to slide limp on the cold stone ground.

As this took place, [[@Splatts]] and [[@Ichoren]] wasted no time in searching for their companions. They rushed through the mansion, and soon found a set of doors that led down into tunnels beneath the building. On a hunch, they went down into the darkness, finding themselves in a dark, misty dungeon. [[@Ichoren]] burst through every door they came across as [[@Splatts]] struggled to keep up.

Soon they heard some kind of chanting, and ignoring that for the time being, came across a person eating alone on the instructions of [[Amelia Darkscale]]. They realised she was eating leftovers from their meal the night before. Leaving her to her fate, they moved on, and found [[@Jack]] and [[@Zharek]] stood over the unconscious Marcus.

So began their rampage. They executed Marcus, and made their way towards the chanting, hoping to find [[@Jack]] and [[@Zharek]]'s belongings and some clues to stop the mists.

Then thy came across [[Amelia Darkscale]], chanting to a large statue of a female lizard-kin. With her were **Brenda** and **Min**.

[[@Ichoren]] was not in the mood for conversation - he punched her right away, knocking her to the ground.

As Brenda stepped forward to chastise them for violating the sanctity of the chamber to [[Velthira]], [[@Jack]] stabbed her in the neck. He interrogated her as she lay dying for directions, and when she had expired, [[@Ichoren]] took the knife to [[Amelia Darkscale]] as well.

[[@Splatts]] spoke to Min, and through various yes/no questions was able to discern her brother had died but that there was a sacrificial ritual taking place and a gemstone was involved. First, they chose to go after their belongings, not least so [[@Zharek]] had his spell book.

Nearby they found **Jamar**, and just beyond him a chest containing their items. Despite his attempts to talk his way out of it, they sent Jamar to the same fate as the other members of [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] who had made the mistake of capturing them. 

In one room they came across huge roots from a tree that has grown down through the dungeon's ceiling. They oozed a bluish sap. [[@Zharek]] tried to examine it, but was unfamiliar. [[@Jack]] tasted the sap, and found himself breathing mist. 

Onwards they went towards the sacrificial chamber, following the sound of more chanting.

They came to a huge, sea cavern that was connected to the dungeon. The water extended out into the darkness, and spanning out across they was a wooden pier. At the end of this, cage and afraid, was a frog-person. Stood on the pier before him, and facing out towards the water holding a cloak pin set with a black gemstone, was an elderly female. She chanted, "Rise oh Deep One, come forth, I summon you! Bless this one!"

Without hesitation, [[@Ichoren]] activated his Buckler of Magnetism and wrenched the cloak pin from her hands to into his. Then he charged her down, but she was too fast and span like a matador, causing [[@Ichoren]] to run off the pier and into the water. Thankfully, [[@Zharek]] reacted quickly and used Bounce to teleport himself, catch [[@Ichoren]], and bring them both back to the shore in the blink of an eye. 

Yet the reprieve was short-lived, for a terrified squeal alerted them to the huge, black tentacle that had emerged from the water and coiled itself around the frog-person's cage...

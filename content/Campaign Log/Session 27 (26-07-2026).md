*Luminar 115 (Morning), Veilhold*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]]

[[@Jack]] assessed his surroundings. He was in a dark, stone chamber, his hands bound with rope and mouth gagged, and [[@Zharek]] lay unconscious beside him. In the gloom of his surroundings he could make out several empty cages and bones scattered around.

For a moment he thought he was in luck, his lockpicks were still hidden within his clothing, until he realised they were useless to help him escape from his rope bindings. He managed to move himself around the room and came across a rusty sword that he used to free himself from his bonds. 

Using the soggy sock that had gagged him, he woke [[@Zharek]] by rubbing it in his face. As both collected themselves, they heard footsteps approach from beyond a closed doorway. They rushes either side of the entrance and waited to ambush whoever came in.

**Marcus**, one of the congregants from the previous night's meal, entered. They quickly set upon him, seizing their chance to escape. [[@Zharek]] struck him hard. [[@Jack]] jumped in and attempted to grapple him but Marcus was too fast; he turned the tables, taking [[@Jack]] in a choke hold from the rear. He wheeled around, buying himself space and using [[@Jack]] as a shield. However, [[@Jack]] soon managed to free himself. In response, Marcus pulled out his dagger and adopted a defensive stance, keeping one eye on the doorway. [[@Ichoren]] rushed him and managed to disarm him, causing the knife to clatter to the ground. 

When the moment arose, Marcus rushed for the doorway. [[@Zharek]] managed to land another blow and [[@Jack]] picked up the dagger and slashed wildly at him causing a wound to his back. As Marcus fled down the corridor, [[@Zharek]] plucked a skull from the ground and launched it towards him, striking him on the head and knocking him clean out. Marcus fell limp to the cold stone ground, sliding to a stop.

As this took place, [[@Splatts]] and [[@Ichoren]] wasted no time in searching for their companions. They rushed through the mansion and soon found a set of doors that led down into tunnels beneath the building. On a hunch, they went down into the darkness, finding themselves in a dark, misty dungeon. [[@Ichoren]] burst through every door they came across as [[@Splatts]] struggled to keep up.

Soon they heard some kind of chanting, and ignoring that for the time being, came across a person eating alone at a table. She told them she was told to by [[Amelia Darkscale]]. They realised she was eating leftovers from their meal the night before. Leaving her to her fate, they moved on, and found [[@Jack]] and [[@Zharek]] stood over the unconscious Marcus in the adjoining passageway.

So began their rampage. They executed Marcus, and made their way towards the chanting, hoping to find [[@Jack]] and [[@Zharek]]'s belongings and some clues to stop the mists.

Then they came across [[Amelia Darkscale]] who knelt chanting before a large statue of a female lizard-kin. With her were **Brenda** and **Min**.

[[@Ichoren]] was not in the mood for conversation - he punched her right away, knocking her to the ground.

Brenda stepped forward to chastise them for violating the sanctity of the chamber of [[Velthira]].[[@Jack]] stabbed her in the neck. As she lay dying, he interrogated her for directions, and when she had expired, [[@Ichoren]] took the knife to [[Amelia Darkscale]] as well.

[[@Splatts]] spoke to Min, and through various yes/no questions was able to discern her brother had died but that there was a sacrificial ritual taking place and a gemstone was involved. First, they chose to go after their belongings, not least so [[@Zharek]] had his spell book.

Nearby they found **Jamar**, and in the room beyond him a chest containing their items. Despite his attempts to talk his way out of it, they sent Jamar to the same fate as the other members of [[Church of Velthira|The Church]] who made the mistake of capturing them. 

As they searched the dungeon, they came across huge roots from a tree that had grown down through the ceiling. The tree oozed a bluish sap. [[@Zharek]] tried to examine it but was unfamiliar. [[@Jack]] tasted the sap, and found himself breathing mist. 

Onwards they went towards the sacrificial chamber, following the sound of more chanting.

They came to a huge, underground sea cave. The water extended out into darkness, and spanning out into it was a long wooden pier. At the end of this, hung in a cage, was a terrified frog-person. Stood on the pier before him was an elderly female. She faced out towards the water holding a cloak pin set with a black gemstone up into the air. She bellowed words, "Rise oh Deep One, come forth, I summon you! Bless this one!"

Without hesitation, [[@Ichoren]] activated his Buckler of Magnetism and wrenched the cloak pin from her hands to into his. Then he charged her down, but she was too fast and span like a matador, causing him to run off the pier and fall towards the water. Thankfully, [[@Zharek]] reacted quickly and used Bounce to teleport himself, catch [[@Ichoren]] mid-air, and bring them both back to the shore in the blink of an eye. 

The reprieve was short-lived. A terrified squeal alerted them to the huge, black tentacle that had emerged from the water and coiled itself around the frog-person's cage...

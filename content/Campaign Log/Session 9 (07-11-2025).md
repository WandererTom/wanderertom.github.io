*Luminar 102 (Afternoon), Morenthia*  
[[@Jack]], [[@Ichoren]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Zharek]]

The party went back to the [[Watchman's Rest]] where they met the civil servant as planned. In a discreet corner of the busy tavern, the servant—**Wylan "Fred" Werrit**—passed them several packages under the table, each containing a cloak of the style worn by the clerks and servants of [[City Hall]] and emblazoned with the hall's motif. The party took the opportunity to ask him a few more questions about the general layout of the building. He also told them they could provide his name to the guards as the person they were working for to help them gain access, albeit he would deny knowledge of them if they were caught out. [[@Jack]] bought him a large drink which he downed in one to settle his nerves, and then he scurried out of the tavern as he watched over his shoulder cautious of observers.

After some discussion, the party decided to simply enter [[City Hall]] through the front door—they had a right to be there after all (in their disguise). Although they were stopped by the guards on the steps, it didn't take too much effort to convince them they were arriving for work, and [[@Ichoren]]'s inane babble about "form C24" and various other paperwork largely aided his efforts to avoid suspicion.

Inside, the party found themselves in a large foyer and decided to split up, sticking in the pairs they had formed when entering. [[@Jack]] and [[@Splatts]] went left, taking the western corridor. [[@Ichoren]] and [[@Zharek]] climbed the stairs up to the first floor.

As [[@Splatts]] and [[@Jack]] walked the corridors they casually passed by several guards, and offered a greeting and some small-talk to allay suspicion. They eventually found themselves outside the Judge's Chambers, and [[@Jack]] confidently flung open the door and entered the room. Inside he found [[Denarin Varn]] sat at a large desk, mid-conversation with three guests—[[Lorien Shade]], the head of the Dockmaster's Guild, [[Blackbeak]], an angry looking mallard with a distinctive black beak, who wore a black pirate hat and long red coat, and another weasel-looking halfling guildmaster. [[Denarin Varn]] was angered by the intrusion, particularly as he had distinctly asked not to be disturbed. [[@Jack]] did not hesitate to lay the blame on Fred for failing to pass on the message, and [[Denarin Varn|Varn]] made it clear Fred would be "dealt with" for his incompetence. As [[@Jack]] made his apologies and retreated from the room, he eyed the papers on [[Denarin Varn|Varn's]] desk and observed one contained lists of nobles houses.

Meanwhile, upstairs [[@Ichoren]] and [[@Zharek]] talked their way past a weary guard positioned the staircase by convincing him that somehow [[@Zharek]]—being new and foolish—had left some important papers in the chambers upstairs. Tired and annoyed by their presence, the guard allowed them to pass, but told them to make it quick and—whatever they did—not to wake the sleeping beast. As they entered the upper chambers, they were greeted by the loud sound of snoring. The pair peeked into the room and found they had arrived at a large bedchamber occupied by a very ugly and very mean looking ogre—the source of the snoring. [[@Ichoren]] crept across the room and quietly lifted several items of paperwork from atop a writing desk, including a bound journal (that he could not read himself). As he crept back, one of the ogre's beady eyes opened and sleepily tracked him. The ogre launched from his large rocking chair, wielding a huge club, but [[@Zharek]]—prepared for the eventuality—immediately used "Flick" to create a loud distraction behind the ogre and caused it to turn in confusion, and bought them both valuable time to escape. A loud, furious roar followed them down the stairs as they rushed passed the now panicked guard. [[@Zharek]] and [[@Ichoren]] wasted no time in fleeing [[City Hall]] and headed back to the pub for a well-earned drink.

[[@Jack]] and [[@Splatts]] had, however, learned to be somewhat more discreet and for a while listened at the door of the Judge's Chambers. They overheard conversations between [[Denarin Varn]] and his guests. [[Blackbeak]] was angry and somewhat hostile towards [[Denarin Varn|Varn]], but was calmy reminded of his subservience by veiled threats from [[Denarin Varn|Varn]] to involve "those above" him. [[Lorien Shade]] reported the Dockmasters had seized all ships in the docks and were levying "emergency" taxes—as requested—on any ships that landed. [[Denarin Varn|Varn]] was pleased, and impressed upon [[Blackbeak]] the need to maintain control of the river and prevent any ships leaving without [[Denarin Varn|Varn's]] knowledge. As the conversation ended, [[@Jack]] and [[@Splatts]] ducked into the nearby courtroom to avoid bumping into the guests as they left. They remained in court for a while, and watched as an unfortunate soul was handed down a lengthy prison sentence for breaching the city's curfew and then dragged out of court by guards. 

Eventually, [[@Jack]] and [[@Splatts]] left the courtroom and found Fred in his nearby office. They suggested it would be a good time for Fred to take some holiday time and impressed upon him the need to leave, explaining [[@Jack]]'s faux pas in the Judge's Chambers that may have landed Fred in a spot of bother with [[Denarin Varn]].

Soon after, [[@Jack]] and [[@Splatts]] made their way back to the foyer intending to leave [[City Hall]]. They came across [[Denarin Varn]] and his ogre who were chastising the guard who had failed in his duty to guard the stairs. [[Denarin Varn|Varn]] had discovered his bedchamber had been entered and several documents were stolen from his desk. The ogre described the culprits. As [[Denarin Varn|Varn]] called for the alarm to be sounded, several guards surrounded [[@Jack]] and [[@Splatts]] who upon seeing this had attempted to flee the other way but been noticed. However, [[@Splatts]] quickly spun a yarn to convince [[Denarin Varn|Varn]] they had merely returned to help sound the alarm, and—aided by the fact they did not have the same appearance as the intruders his ogre had seen—he believed and released them. 

[[City Hall]] went into lockdown, and [[@Jack]] and [[@Splatts]] decided to take advantage of their apparently convincing deception and wait it out. 

Meanwhile, [[@Zharek]] had by now read the journal [[@Ichoren]] had stolen from [[Denarin Varn]]'s desk. A recent entry detailed [[Denarin Varn|Varn's]] lust for power, and that his allegiance to "those in the shadows" was a small price to pay. He wrote about how they had grown to trust him, and he was keeping what they had sent him safe and secret nearby. He believed he would soon be accepted by them fully.

In this knowledge, and concerned by the time that had elapsed without [[@Splatts]] and [[@Jack]] re-appearing, [[@Zharek]] returned to [[City Hall]] to assess the situation his friends found themselves in. He saw there was now significant activity by the guards on the lower floor, and so he took advantage of the distraction and discreetly flew onto the rooftop garden and then peeked through the doors to get a view of the main staircase. He saw the guard on the stairs had been called down, and could hear the ogre's voice bellow from the foyer below also. He darted back up into [[Denarin Varn]]'s bedchamber to have a good look around while it was empty.

Despite acting quickly to avoid detection, [[@Zharek]] conducted an inspired search of the rooms. He discovered a space on the upper level that he could not account for; there was a room-sized void but no obvious passage into it. This knowledge helped him identify a secret door that was built into a statue on an adjoining bathroom wall. A hidden lever revealed a secret vault, and as the door swung open he found himself bathed in the gentle yellow reflection of an abundance of gold coins and other riches.

[[@Zharek]] frantically grabbed a few items that jumped out to his attention:
- A handful of gold coins (20gp worth) from a very large chest that overflowed with gold ("no one will notice a small amount missing", he thought).
- An ornate brass key that was on a table.
- A fancy looking hand mirror that was on a table (he later discovered this had magical properties).
- A painting that depicted a Karkion dressed in flowing purple and gold filigree robes, and who harnessed a stream of magical energy, signed by **Kiri Nug** (he ripped this from its frame and rolled it up).
- A small scroll that was on the table.

Inside the vault he also noticed several other items including several expensive looking gemstones and a golden statue head of a dragon, with a plaque below it that read "*Sol-Ra the Treacherous.*" He was limited by what he could carry.

He decided not to return the way he came but instead dramatically flew out of an upper floor window. In doing so he drew the attention of the guards below him, and they futilely fired crossbow bolts at him as he disappeared into the clouds above.

When the party regrouped at the [[Watchman's Rest]], they discreetly read the scroll that [[@Zharek]] had taken and found it to be a letter addressed to [[Denarin Varn]].

> Denarin,
> 
> The veil of order has been torn. We must act without hesitation and seize the opportunity presented. Your place within what comes next is pivotal—we trust your response will please us. 
> 
> Mayor Alron Noon has taken an unexpected—and permanent—leave of absence. Most will assume him fled and you will perpetuate this fantasy. You must act immediately and step into the void with a hand of iron. Make what was his yours—the city’s heart must beat to your command.
> 
> We have supplied you with means to secure the loyalty of the Shining Daggers—use them as your teeth and claws. The guildmasters you will find also easily bound with coin or compelled by fear; they will fall in line once you hold the reins. Any noble who dares raise claim will be silenced at our hand—if necessary remind them what lurks in shadow and bites unseen.
> 
> The tides will shift again soon. When those of red skin come knocking at your gates, you will receive instruction from us. Until then, maintain control. Crush dissent. Rule without compromise.
> 
> We send also a key—and with it our trust and symbol of our word. When your task is complete in Morenthia, this key will grant you passage into the domain of our Everwood brood. If we are pleased, your request of initiation will be granted. The shadows await, do not disappoint.
> 
> Remember: stones may crack, but their halves are strongest when sundered.
> 
> — Stamped with the symbol of a stone split in two.

The party contemplated the letter and discerned they now had the evidence the [[Beaky Blinders]] had sought. They decided their next steps were to take it to them and then make their move to escape the city and continue on their journey to [[Alzar's Tower]]...

*Luminar 105 (Evening), Alzar's Tower*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]]

Having recuperated and taken some time to rest, the party prepared to leave [[Alzar's Tower]] and journey to the [[Everwood]] in their endeavour to seek out [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]]'s base.

In parting, [[@Jack]] asked [[Uthimial]] where he might get his sword repaired (the broken blade he found in the [[Um’brathir]] ship). [[Uthimial]] suggested he find the blacksmith **Edward** in [[Eldergrove]], commonly known as "Eldergrove Edward".

[[Uthimial]] asked of their plans, and suggested the party looked out for the [[Faewynn Leafshade|Wistful Warden]] if they got lost, although she could only be seen in moonlight - unless they had another means of seeing her. He said there was not much that passes through the forest without her knowing of it.

After being informed about the teleporter, several of the party stepped onto the eye symbol and were transported back to the base of the tower in a blink. [[@Ichoren]] sent [[@Jack]] back up in jest, and [[@Zharek]] opted to use his own means of transport and fly down from the window instead.

The group walked back to their boat, conscious it was already late in the evening, intending to sail over to Lake Omen overnight. Although [[@Splatts]] led the way, he became confused as darkness fell, but [[@Jack]] managed to pick up the path with his instincts and get them back safely.

In the relative safety of their ship, the party discussed their plan. [[@Ichoren]] spoke more about his time with [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]], confessing that he was - in fact - created by them. He removed his gloves to reveal iridescent ink-like runes and swirls on his hands ([[@Zharek]] sensed this was something close to Elementalism). He explained he had only been "born" shortly before he met [[@Splatts]], and had escaped from the [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]] with the help of a dwarf called [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]] - his creator. He didn't need to sleep or eat either.

He had one final, particularly big confession: his heart was the fragment of the [[Spark of Asantia]], and it was this that imbued him with his lifeforce.

While several of the party were stunned by the revelations, [[@Splatts]] immediately hugged [[@Ichoren]].

[[@Jack]] was flummoxed, and while has hand unconsciously drifted to the hilt of his sword, internally he realised this news had up-ended everything he thought he wanted and changed his perspective on the fabled gemstone entirely. He no longer coveted it in the same way - knowing now that part of it was part of [[@Ichoren]] - albeit the major part of the stone was still unaccounted for. 

[[@Ichoren]] offered the fragment to [[@Jack]] if he ever died. [[@Splatts]] made sure to insist this shouldn't be an "untimely death". 

[[@Ichoren]] and [[@Zharek]] bonded over their love of punching things with their magical hands.

The party were reluctant to take [[@Ichoren]] back into potential danger, back to [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]]. But they knew this was likely the best way forward, and he hoped it would help them find their way into [[Asantia's Blessing]].

Having relieved himself of his secrets, [[@Ichoren]] kept watch as the party rested on the ship. The night closed in, cold and still, fires burning on the horizon. The air was heavy and ominous.

At first light the party set out from their ship, docked on the southern edge of Lake Omen, and walked into the [[Everwood]]. He they entered the vast expanse of trees, filled with huge, ancient oaks and leaves that shifted and whispered. They took the main road towards [[Eldergrove]], and after several hours of walking they arrived at a crossroads. [[@Ichoren]] recognised he had been there before, and walked from the beaten path into the thick forest. As he took the time to sense his surroundings, he realised he was close to [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]] base that he'd previously fled. With daylight fading, they decided to head down to [[Eldergrove]] for the night.

They found [[Eldergrove]] was a wondrous place, a woodland realm in the trees. Several solid buildings were on the ground, but most of the town was raised high above, a collection of huge treehouses joined by bridges and accessible by winding stairs that spiralled thick trees. They passed a general store on the ground, and the clanging of metal upon metal quickly drew them to the smithy.

Here they found Edward Grove, the local blacksmith. [[@Jack]] asked him to fix his broken sword, and he agreed, although he was also very keen to buy it from him. [[@Jack]] persuaded him to repair it, paying extra to have the work done as soon as possible.

Afterwards, the party rested at the **Fox & Squirrel** tavern high up in the trees, sleeping in hammocks that swung out into the open air between the trees. [[@Ichoren]] pointedly asked for a reinforced hammock for himself.

The next morning, having breakfasted, the party head down to the [[Hollow Archive]] where they met [[Ysaria]]. She was perched on a branch high inside. She welcomed them, curious to see another Karkion in her presence. [[@Zharek]] asked for information on [[Caldurax]], and she agree to tell him what she knew, but only in exchange for a memory from each of them - one they would give up forever. [[@Zharek]], [[@Splatts]] and [[@Jack]] agreed, although [[@Ichoren]] thought his memories were too dangerous to release. She asked them to speak a memory not spoken of before, and in the process extracted it from their minds as luminescent strings of light, placing each carefully into a bottle and then onto her shelves. 

- [[@Zharek]] spoke of his last memory of his parents, when he had graduated top of his magic class. Everyone said he was a genius, and his parents said they loved him. It was the last time he saw them.
- [[@Splatts]] talked of a warm memory of spending all day outside playing with his siblings, getting into mischief, and then getting called in for dinner. At the table he talked with his family about his ideas for a new shop, and that he wanted to earn money to support them. The next day he set out on his great adventure.
- [[@Jack]] told a darker tale, his oldest memory. He was six triads old, and had just come home from school excited to see his father who had been working in his gem shop all week. He went into the shop, the bell ringing out above the door, and found his father leaning over a cabinet of sparling gems. [[@Jack]] said, "Daddy Wolfskin, please come and play with me". His father stared back at him in disgust. "You don't have time for playing you stupid little cretin," he said, "get out there and earn some money". He gave [[@Jack]] a small hammer, which he took to find geodes in the forest, spending the next hours smashing them up looking for gems with tearful eyes and bloodied hands. 

Impressed with the valuable memories the party had sacrificed, [[Ysaria]] gave [[@Zharek]] information he had sought. He asked specifically about a grimoire that was lost when [[Caldurax]] fell.

She said legend had it that a great grimoire was lost at that time - known as the [[Lost Grimoire of Caldurax]]. This was written by [[Magister Elarion]] in the kingdom's capital, [[Varnhold]], shortly before it crashed to the ground after the magics holding it were destroyed in [[The Rending]]. Some say the grimoire was buried deep under the ruins of the fallen city.

The kingdom's last ruler was named [[King Ruiandril]], or **King Rui** as he often referred to himself. He was served by several mages headed by the **Royal Thaumaturge**. The [[Caldurax|Calduraxian]] people had a deep love and fascination with magic, and were far more adept with it than the other nations, rumoured to have combined streams of magic into one. [[Caldurax]] was a strictly neutral nation and not embroiled in the war, albeit fell terrible victim to it.

She knew [[Magister Elarion]]'s ward managed to flee [[Caldurax]] before it fell, and he carried with him a medallion infused with great magic - a symbol of the royal mages. The ward was known as [[Zian]], and he - in fact - went on to teach [[Archmage Veylin]], [[@Zharek]]'s own mentor.

[[Ysaria]] knew [[Archmage Veylin]] personally and was saddened when [[@Zharek]] informed her of his passing.

After this conversation, [[@Ichoren]] asked [[Ysaria]] if she had seen [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]], describing her appearance. She explained she had not seen many dwarves around there, but some did come every few weeks to purchase supplies. They did not interact with the townsfolk. She believed she had recently seen a young redhead but couldn't be sure. 

The party left [[Ysaria]] with their thanks and headed back up to the village. [[@Jack]] collected his newly repaired sword as [[@Ichoren]] went back into the forest in the hope of spotting some activity by [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]].

After a while he noticed a person short of stature emerge from the bushes, wearing in a dark hooded cloak. Then their eyes met...

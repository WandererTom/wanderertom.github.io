*Luminar 103 (Evening), Nordvale*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Zharek]]

Perched on the rooftop as dusk set in, [[@Zharek]] intently watched the house [[Ozon Fallcrock]] had entered for any sign of activity. After a few minutes, he saw another person—a thin human male in his 30's— walk briskly down the alleyway towards the house and repeatedly look back over his shoulders as if wary of being seen. The man knocked quietly on the front door of [[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]]'s house and, when the door opened, checked again for observers before he quickly entered.

[[@Zharek]] flew down to the ground and decided he would casually walk past the house to see if he could get a view in through the large bay window. In doing so he saw [[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]] and the male deep in conversation, but an untimely stumble on a step drew their attention and [[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]] turned to look out. His eyes met with [[@Zharek]]'s. [[@Zharek]] attempted to walk of nonchalantly to allay suspicion, he heard the sound of curtains being drawn across the window and realised the opportunity to spy had gone. He decided to make his way back to the rest of the group at the Town Hall. 

There [[@Zharek]] relayed what he'd seen and the group asked Mikey if he knew the identity of the man described. He said it was likely **Elanralang'athriel**, [[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]]'s trusted right-hand man. The group asked for someone to go and keep watch near the house for any other activity and so **Donny** made his way over to take up position.

While [[@Ichoren]] got to work and helped plan some of the town's defences, [[@Zharek]] took to the skies hoping to get a view of the war camp they'd heard about. He settled high on the nearby spire of the local Church of Asantia, and cast his vision out westwards across the valley using his Far Sight. 

As he projected himself into the sky roughly 10 kilometers due west, [[@Zharek]] found his incorporeal form floating above the war camp. He saw the invaders had occupied a farmstead and taken over the surrounding land. Row after row of perfectly aligned tents were arranged with military precision. Wispy columns of spoke marked the locations of firepits that provided light and warmth around the camp. Numerous [[Um’brathir]] soldiers busied themselves around the camp—pairs patrolled both inside and the perimeter, some cooked food on campfires, some honed weapons or gathered supplies, and others guarded the entrances to several larger tents that stood tall above the others. No soldier was idle, this was a camp that was disciplined and organised. [[@Zharek]] saw floating high in the air were two black air balloons, tethered to the ground inside the camp by long ropes. Beneath them hung large baskets, and within these were soldiers who kept watch over the camp and the surrounding lands. Wooden gated barricades marked the entrances to the camp on either side. 

[[@Zharek]] returned his consciousness to his physical form and flew down from his vantage point to tell the group what he'd seen. Whilst doing so he noticed Donny was running towards the Town Hall whilst he anxiously looked behind him. The reason became obvious, for a short distance behind [[Ozon Fallcrock]] walked with purpose up the high street, flanked by **Elanralang'athriel** and roughly ten guards armed with spears. They were headed directly for the Town Hall. [[@Zharek]] stayed in the air and discreetly watched from above.

When he reached the hall, Donny burst inside to warn [[@Ichoren]] and [[@Splatts]] of the impending trouble. [[@Ichoren]] wanted to address [[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]] head on and walked out to stand in the street and meet him, arms folded. [[@Splatts]] joined him and prepared himself for action. 

As both parties faced off, [[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]] commanded his loyal guards to surround [[@Ichoren]] and [[@Splatts]] and arrest them. He said they had taken him for a fool, and he did not believe that a lowly carpenter was allegedly now a Count, had survived being eaten by a sea monster, and any of the other tales they had spun. The only logical explanation was they were infiltrators sent by the enemy.

In response, [[@Ichoren]] calmly but directly called out [[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]] and announced that if he sincerely believed they worked for the enemy then he should just lay down his arms and surrender to them now, for that was what he advocated the town should do anyway. [[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]] was taken aback by the challenge and struggled to argue against it. As he stumbled with his words, it allowed [[@Splatts]] a window of opportunity to appeal directly to the guards. Seeing his friend Steven within the group, and knowing many faces as customers of his shop, he persuaded the guards they could be trusted and had no reason to lie. Many of the guards murmured in agreement. Seeing his attempt to take charge was drastically failing, [[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]] conceded his position and instructed the guards to lower their weapons. He explained how he had just wanted to save as many lives as he could against what he believed was an unbeatable enemy, but he was out of his depth. He agreed to follow [[@Ichoren]]'s plan and help prepare for an attack, particularly after [[@Ichoren]] explained how they'd seen first hand that the [[Um’brathir]] do not take captives (with one exception, [[Commander Eladar Squinketon|Eladar]]).  

The conversation turned to planning. [[@Ichoren]] asked [[Ozon Fallcrock|Ozon]] what he knew of the [[Bladedancers]], and heard they were a mercenary group made up mainly of retired [[Valeguard]] and [[Queensguard]] veterans. Although they took paid work, they operated ethically and often helped those in need. They were based south-east of the [[Everwood]] near **Ombrin's Beacon**, and had setup a permanent watch there due to their long-term vow to keep lookout for any signs of trouble from the [[Disputed Lands]].  

[[@Ichoren]] suggested that someone set out immediately to find the [[Bladedancers]] and seek their assistance to defend the town. Mikey volunteered, and [[@Ichoren]] gave him [[Lord Timbrel Toodlepip|Lord Toodlepip]]'s signet ring in the hope this would help persuade them. 

[[@Zharek]] asked around and found out he could source explosives from the nearby **Easthill Mines**, having formed various plans to delay or disrupt the invading force. At his request, two guards set off to collect the items and said they'd take about a day to return.

Whilst the townspeople busily enacted the group's plans, the trio decided to visit [[@Splatts]]' shop, but not before they had enthusiastically egged the windows of [[@Jack]]'s shop on the way.

When they arrived, front door of [[@Splatts]]' shop was ajar, and [[@Ichoren]] noticed it had been forced open. Inside, they found the shop was completely ransacked, but strangely nothing was obviously missing—it looked like someone had conducted a quick and very untidy search. Upstairs, the living quarters were in a similarly disturbed state. As [[@splatts]] looked around he noticed something that stood out—a black dagger had been thrust into his coffee table, and had pinned to it two pieces of paper.

He removed the dagger and examined the papers. The first was a letter addressed to him from his family, written in his mother's hand. It explained that [[@Splatts]]' father had discovered a deposit of valuable rare minerals in the hills. They had left [[Nordvale]] to find a place in the countryside close to this, and away from danger and—they hoped—to find some peace. They told [[@Splatts]] that he didn’t need to worry about them, though if he chose he could come and stay with them and they would like that very much. However, they understood he had his business and they wanted him to follow his dreams, but they also desperately wanted him to be safe. As they expressed their love for him the letter ended abruptly—incomplete. The final word trailed off and turned into a scrawl that ran down the page...

Beneath this letter was another much shorter note, this one written in a deliberate, fine handwriting. It read, "**We have your beloved. Deliver the orb to Tu'nosdoth by The Day of Long Shadows, else they die.**" This note was marked with a symbol, one that [[@Zharek]] immediately recognised as identical to that tattooed on the red-skinned figure who had watched them from the riverbank...

![[Tunosdoth Symbol.png|100]]  

[[@Splatts]] was distraught at this news and terrified for the safety of his family. Eventually he composed himself and started to think logically. He knew the *Day of Long Shadows* was on Luminar 117—about 14 days away—so he had time to make a plan. He questioned aloud how the [[Um’brathir]] knew he had [[The Emberglass|The Orb]] and where to find him. [[@Zharek]] reminded him that the [[Um’brathir]] had been watching them, probably for a while.

[[@Splatts]] took the black knife from the table and vowed to use it to avenge his family's kidnap. [[@Zharek]] gifted him his book on knife fighting to help improve his skills.

[[@Splatts]] collected some paper and created leaflets to encourage the locals to join the effort to defend the town and fight the invaders. As he handed them out the response was significant, and the town began to buzz with hope.

Exhausted by the day's events, [[@Splatts]] and [[@Zharek]] rested. [[@Ichoren]] took a stretch rest but decided to head out to help the townspeople construct more defences. 

For [[@Splatts]], the night was restless and filled with dreams of his family—vivid images of his mother, father, brother, and sister. His dreams were haunted by the questions that faced him. Where were they? Were they together? Were they OK? Then a vision of his mother approached him, holding out the glowing red [[The Emberglass|Orb]]. [[@Splatts]] took it and found himself once again enveloped entirely in its warm, red glow as the world around him tuned out. The same gentle, female voice spoke to him, disembodied, ethereal and all around him. She acknowledged [[@Splatts]] had suffered great loss and the challenges that faced him, but reminded him to consider the greater issues at stake. She feared the entire Kingdom could fall, but the goddesses had chosen [[@Splatts]] for a reason. [[@Splatts]] asked the voice if she knew the place "Tu'nosdoth", and she encouraged him to open his mind and consider whether this name belonged to a place or a person. Overwhelmed by the situation, [[@Splatts]] expressed he was torn and didn't know what to do for the best, but he desperately wanted to save his family. The voice reminded [[@Splatts]] he was not alone, and to trust in his heart and his companions.

[[@Splatts]] woke to the flickering candlelight of his bedroom, and as he got up he noticed [[The Emberglass|The Orb]] glowed but then faded.

Downstairs, [[@Splatts]] told [[@Zharek]] about the dream. Then they both went out to find [[@Ichoren]] who had worked through the night to build defences outside the front gate of the town. As the group discussed events, a shout drew their attention to two riders on horseback who rapidly approached from the valley. On the back of one of the horses was a third figure, an [[Um’brathir]] soldier who was bound and gagged. "Open the gates!" called one of the riders, "We've got one!"

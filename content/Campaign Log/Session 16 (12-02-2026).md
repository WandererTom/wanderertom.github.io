*Luminar 107 (Morning), Eldergrove*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Splatts]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Cove-Candle]]

[[@Ichoren]] and the cloaked dwarf stared at each other. That was until she accused him of spying on her going about her innocent business in the forest. Unphased by the challenge, [[@Ichoren]] managed to convince her he was a friend of [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]]. He handed over his knitted hat, asking the dwarf to take it to her as a sign he was there. 

The dwarf - **Zerin Sugafree** - said she could not get to [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]] because she'd been confined to the caves ever since "the incident". [[@Ichoren]] was able to figure out that Zerin was another reluctant member of [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]], and she was a friend of [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]]. She agreed to accompany him for a short time to [[Eldergrove]] to speak with his friends.

There they questioned her further, finding out something was going on in the caves but she wasn't sure what. She told them they'd need a key to access the base, and described the rough layout as far as she knew it. She also told them the Grand Master had arrived for a meeting of the council, so if they were going to sneak it that might provide distraction.

The party decided to try and find another entrance to the base, but not waste too much time - they wanted to take advantage of the natural distraction of the meeting. [[@Ichoren]] found his way to the base entrance, and they ultimately entered the main doorway using the key [[@Zharek]] had stolen from [[Denarin Varn]].

Inside, the party negotiated a trapped corridor without issue and found themselves inside the base proper. It was an eerie series of corridors lit with soft red light and humming with the sound of machinery. Pipework protruded from many of the walls.

They made their way discreetly through the near-empty corridors, and [[@Splatts]] managed to bluff his way past one lower-level member by convincing him he was a new initiate. Eventually, they came to the caves entrance.

Inside, they encountered a large workshop of sorts, occupied by a scientist, his apprentice, and a strange-looking lady sculpting figures out of clay. [[@Ichoren]] wasted no time and threw the scientist to the floor, demanding to know where [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]] was. He said she was in the adjoining rooms. [[@Zharek]] tied the others up whilst [[@Ichoren]] dragged to the scientist to his feet and forced him to take him to [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]].

In the next chamber, the party discovered a strange arrangement of large glass capsules, all except one containing the remnants of a creation appearing exactly like [[@Ichoren]].

[[@Zharek]] found a log book describing experiments to bring life to the creatures, reporting that nearly all failed. One record described success, but the "child" escaped soon after.

[[@Jack]] searched and found numerous discarded gemstones around, seemingly used as experiments to power the creatures. He placed a few in his pockets for safe keeping. 

[[@Ichoren]] went into the next chamber to find a smaller version of the one he'd come from, containing three capsules. [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]] was here,  overwhelmed to see him. She questioned why he'd returned. [[@Ichoren]] discovered something that horrified him, and made him question [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]]'s motivations - she had created another construct, this time a female.

[[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]] explained she was doing so under duress. She had tried to delay [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]] but she could only do so for so long. They knew she was the only one who had successfully created a "child", and had confined her to the caves to create more. They had only achieved true success using the fragment of the [[Spark of Asantia]]; the gem that powered [[@Ichoren]]. She doubted the female would live without another fragment, but she couldn't leave her. She went on to explain that the Grand Master was desperate above all else to find the [[Spark of Asantia]] so they could raise an army - she didn't know for what purpose. He had sent an expedition into [[Asantia's Blessing]] to find it, using information they had extracted from a prisoner to gain entry, but they had not yet returned.

[[@Ichoren]] approached the capsule, seeing a female version of himself inside. She reacted to his presence, pressing her hand to his against the glass. Then he did something dangerous. He channeled his own lifeforce and transferred it to her - risking death in the process. It worked, and he imbued her with life and consciousness. 

Having freed "Sistoren", they persuaded [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]] to flee with her using the secret passageway she'd helped [[@Ichoren]] escape through before - a tunnel leading out from the caves. She agreed to head to the party's boat near [[Omen's Deep]] and meet them there.

The party went back into the base to search for information about how to get into [[Asantia's Blessing]].

Ignoring the voices they could hear from around the corner, [[@Splatts]] and [[@Jack]] searched a nearby office, recovering a fine dagger and two documents of note: plans of the great wall (designed by an architect named **Imjan Defthand**), and a map of [[Asantia's Blessing]] with a spot marked with a cross.

As the party regrouped in the cave, [[@Cove-Candle]] heard footsteps approaching...

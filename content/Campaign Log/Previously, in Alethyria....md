It all began at a wedding.

What should have been a joyous celebration at [[Squinketon Manor]] quickly became a nightmare when a vast magical explosion devastated [[Vadoria City]]. Moments later, strange airships descended from the skies, bringing with them an invading force known as the [[Um’brathir]]. The kingdom was under attack, its capital lay in ruins, and [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] had vanished.

In the chaos that followed, you rescued [[Commander Eladar Squinketon]] from the invaders and discovered evidence of a much darker conspiracy involving an organisation known as **[[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone]]**. Your journey led you across [[Alethyria]], from the besieged towns of the west to the mysterious [[Alzar's Tower]], where the great mage [[Uthimial]] revealed the truth behind an ancient artefact: **[[The Emberglass]] orb**.

But the revelations didn't end there. Ichoren finally revealed his own origins: he was not born, but created by [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]], with his life sustained by a fragment of the legendary **[[Spark of Asantia]]**. His creator, [[Brynna Veyl|Brynna]], had helped him escape, and his very existence was tied to the ancient powers you were beginning to uncover.

Your journey brought you into conflict with [[The Cabal of the Sundered Stone|The Cabal]], through forgotten temples, ancient places, and long-dormant portals, and eventually into the [[Nethirian Empire]]. There, you uncovered the treachery of [[Xinia]], a dark sorceress who had imprisoned both [[Emperor Nyin]] and [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]]. You defeated her, freed the monarchs, and helped restore the Emperor to his throne.

In return, you learned the **[[Session 25 (28-06-2026)#^Lore-Twelve-Gemstones-of-the-Dawnmothers-Diadem|lore and locations of the Twelve Gemstones of the Dawnmother's Diadem]]**: twelve immensely powerful stones, each embodying one of [[The Dawnmother]]'s daughters. Scattered across the lands and entrusted to different peoples and kingdoms, these stones were once part of a single artefact of extraordinary power. Should all twelve be reunited, the Diadem could be remade — a prospect that could bring salvation or unimaginable destruction, depending on whose hands held it.

[[Queen Alira Aurelwyn|Queen Alira]] entrusted you with **[[Selarune's Window]]**, believing that you were the right people to safeguard the stones. You already possess **[[Salana's Curse]]**, recovered from Xinia, and the **[[Tear of Gamaliel]]** owned by [[Lord Ronald Squinketon|Lord Squinketon]], and have now set out to find the remaining gemstones.
## Where things stand

There is little time to lose.

**[[Nordvale]] is in grave danger.** When you last visited, the town was desperately trying to prepare for an attack by the [[Um’brathir]]. Its defences were meagre, its [[Valeguard]] soldiers had been called away to the front, and enemy forces were establishing a war camp nearby. The invaders made their intentions abundantly clear: surrender [[The Emberglass]] to their commander, [[Tu'nosdoth]], or [[Nordvale]] would be destroyed.

And the threat is deeply personal. Splatts' family was captured by the [[Um’brathir]] and his father paraded before you as a hostage. His family remains in danger, and the enemy knows exactly how to use that against you.

[[Emperor Nyin]] has pledged his support and promised to raise an army, but marching across the kingdom would take far too long. There may be a way to bring his forces to [[Nordvale]] more quickly, but the unnatural mists of the [[Weeping Deep]] stand in the way. The Emperor believes their source may lie on the [[Isle of Tharos]]. If you can clear the mists, his army may be able to cross and come to [[Nordvale]]'s aid.

And so your journey brought you to that very island.
## The Isle of Tharos

You arrived at [[Veilhold]], a strange settlement shrouded in perpetual mist and twilight. Its inhabitants belong to the [[Church of Velthira]], led by the seemingly welcoming [[Amelia Darkscale]]. But beneath the hospitality lies something sinister.

After being invited to dine with the congregation, you discovered that the Church worships the **Deep One**, a terrible entity associated with [[Velthira]]. Its followers have been conducting sacrifices, and the unnatural mists appear to be connected to their activities.

Your suspicions proved well founded. Jack and Zharek were captured and imprisoned beneath the mansion, whilst Ichoren and Splatts discovered the truth and went in search of their companions.

You eventually reunited and fought your way through the underground chambers, discovering strange roots, mysterious blue sap and evidence of a sacrificial ritual.

You now find yourselves in a vast sea cavern beneath the mansion. Before you stands an elderly woman holding a cloak pin set with a **black gemstone**, chanting to the waters and calling upon the Deep One.

A terrified frog-person is trapped in a cage nearby, and a huge, black tentacle has just emerged from the depths...

**The ritual is underway.**

What happens next is anyone's guess.

But beyond this island, the clock is ticking. [[Nordvale]] awaits its fate, Splatts' family remains in enemy hands, and the future of Alethyria may depend on what you discover amongst the Twelve.

The **Splatt Pack** has work to do.

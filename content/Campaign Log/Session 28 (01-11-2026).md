*Luminar 115 (Morning), Veilhold*  
[[@Ichoren]], [[@Zharek]], [[@Jack]], [[@Splatts]]

The huge, glistening tentacle coiled around the frog's cage. He let out a petrified whimper. Before the party could react, the door slammed shut behind them and locked them inside the chamber. A voice called out as it closed, "You will be blessed by the Deep One!"

Within seconds the poor frog person was pulled down beneath the waves. [[@Ichoren]] wasted no time, and launched himself off the wooden pier and into the water, landing on the dark shape writhing beneath the surface. He attacked furiously as the Deep One started to consume it's prey in a huge maw, then wrestled in a desperate attempt to pull the frog out of its terrible grasp.

Meanwhile, [[@Jack]] slay the cultist, **Semla**, with his longsword. [[@Zharek]] attempted to smash his way out through the locked door using his Power Fist, causing it to buckle slightly but not open.

Seeing the chaos around him, [[@Jack]] roared in rage, inspiring the others to fight hard. [[@Ichoren]] carved into the Deep One with his axe but it was to no avail as the semi-translucent beast swallowed the frog whole. Then it coiled its tentacle around [[@Ichoren]] and pulled him down beneath the surface.

[[@Jack]] dived over and reached down into the cold water, managing to take hold of [[@Ichoren]] and heave him back up. The furious creature responded by surging up and thrust it's razor sharp teeth deep into [[@Ichoren]], leaving him gravely injured. 

[[@Splatts]] kept his wits and searched Selma's body for a key as the terrified **Min** huddled beside him. Finding a key, he shoved open the door, seeing the cultist, Lennon, outside, horrified to see them escaping. 

[[@Jack]] and [[@Ichoren]] fought furiously with the tentacled beast, dodging strikes from it's huge limbs, as [[@Zharek]] - after failing to teleport - set his rage upon **Lennon**. 

[[@Jack]] and [[@Ichoren]] managed to flee to the door, but the creature pursued them. In act of extreme bravery, [[@Splatts]] raised his shield and charged forward to block the beast's attack as it threw its huge bulk to crush them, undoubtedly saving his friends.

Realising the situation was desperate, [[@Ichoren]] lured the creature towards himself and away from his frieds, and was knocked down by another powerful strike. However, his friends managed to drag him out and slammed the door shut behind them, leaving the furious creature to smash against it.

[[@Splatts]] quickly applied his healing salve to [[@Ichoren]], bringing him back from the brink of death. The party collected themselves as the banging on the door subsided and the Deep One returned down into the depths.

Afterwards, [[@Ichoren]] spoke to **Min** and found out about the factory used to create the mists. It was the building they had seen when they arrived at the town down by the shore. They discovered the tree sap was used to create the mist. With this new information, they set about destroying the blue-sapped tree they had found earlier from beneath, and then made their way over to the factory.

In no mood to negotiate, the party forced their way into the factory and immediately started smashing the place up. Any resistance by the workers was futile. The head worker, **Lorus**, approached them, and the party told him they were there to shut the place down. He offered little resistance, expressing he was in many ways pleased because he believed [[Amelia Darkscale]] had taken things too far.

With the factory no longer functioning, the party watched as the mists slowly subsided, revealing the expanse of the [[Weeping Deep]] stretching out before them. They decided to head up to the cliffs for a better view, and keep watch in the hope that [[Emperor of Nethiria|Emperor Nyin]] would stay true to his word and sail to [[Nordvale]]'s aid.

As the day's light began to fade, they made out distant sails appearing on the horizon...

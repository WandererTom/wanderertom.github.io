A trainee Royal Mage of the [[Caldurax|Kingdom of Caldurax]] who fled as the floating cities fell. He sought refuge in the [[Kingdom of Vadoria]].

He was a ward of [[Magister Elarion]] in [[Caldurax]], and ultimately the teacher of [[Archmage Veylin]] in [[Kingdom of Vadoria|Vadoria]].

Human, late 20's (deceased). Tobias was an honored soldier in the [[Queensguard]].

Son of [[Lord Ronald Squinketon]] and younger brother of [[Commander Eladar Squinketon]]. Died several months ago, and the official cause of death was recorded as **Sleeping Fever**.

[[Lord Ronald Squinketon]] has informed the party that his son died in service to [[Queen Alira Aurelwyn]] whilst he was investigating a matter for her.

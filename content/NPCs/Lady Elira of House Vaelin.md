An insufferable noble lady of House Vaelin, more concerned with her bags than the lives of her guards. Aged around 40, human, she is always dressed in fine attire featuring pristine white gloves over which she wears a large signet ring adorned with a bold sapphire. Her fine gown flows to her feet, and a wide-brimmed hat sits atop a head of dark ringlets.

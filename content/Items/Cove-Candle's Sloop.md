
A rare, elegant sloop made of pale wood with fine silver rigging. It is whisper-quiet, swift, and sails well even in low winds, but made from rare elven timber it is difficult to repair.

**Speed:** Fast (150km/day)  
**Crew:** 4+  
**Armour**: 4  
**HP**: 50  

**Traits:** 
- Small to medium size, one mast.
- Stealthy—**boon** to **Sneaking** when attempting to pass quietly.
- Swift—**boon** to **Seamanship** when attempting to cast off and maneuver quickly.
- Delicate—armour has no effect against damage incurred from cannon fire.

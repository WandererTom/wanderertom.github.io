A majestic crown of gleaming gold and silver, woven together in intricacy. Set into its arc are spaces for **twelve gemstones** - one for each of the Dawnmother's precious daughters.

The diadem was gifted to [[The Ancients]] long ago, and - when complete - allowed them to harness the magics of [[Alethyria]]. The diadem was hidden in a temple within [[Asantia's Blessing]] when [[The Ancients]] left the lands. Later, it was discovered by [[The Great Mage of Nethiria]] who attempted to harness it's power and caused [[The Rending]].

In the [[Pact of Ashes]] the diadem was unmade and its gemstones scattered across the lands and hidden away, lest it never be remade again.

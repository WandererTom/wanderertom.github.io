
My faithful servant,

Dire times are upon us, and I must ask of you a task that bears the full weight of the realm. Accompanying this letter is an object of grave and ancient power—an orb unlike any other. You are to deliver it with all haste to **Uthimial**, the mage of **Alzar’s Tower**, who will know what must be done.

You are to speak of this to no one. Trust no one. The safety of the kingdom may well rest upon your swift action and discretion.

Go now, under the light of Asantia. My thoughts and hopes travel with you.

— Your Queen, Alira Aurelwyn, Sovereign of Vadoria

If you read this and are not the one to whom I entrusted this letter, then I must assume that fate, in its cruel design, has forced another to take up this burden. If that soul is you, then know this: you were chosen not by chance, but by providence. See this task done, and you will have the gratitude of the entire Kingdom—and perhaps, more.

Should duty alone not move your heart, I have instructed Uthimial to offer the bearer of this orb a reward befitting the important of this task: a single artefact from his most treasured vaults. He may require a nudge to recall this agreement—mages often forget what they deem trivial—but I assure you, the offer stands. However, if trinkets and treasure weigh heavier than honour and the good of the realm in your estimation, then I must gently urge you to reflect upon your priorities. The world has enough mercenaries, but painfully few heroes.

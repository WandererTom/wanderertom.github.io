The military in the [[Kingdom of Vadoria]], designated to protect the kingdom from hostile threats. Over the years have essentially evolved into law enforcement, primarily used to keep the peace in the kingdom and uphold the law. The Valeguard are primarily based in cities and towns, with the exception of key locations in [[Vadoria City]] that are protected by the [[Queensguard]].

They have a small sub-element designated to patrol the riverways, known as the [[Riverguard]].

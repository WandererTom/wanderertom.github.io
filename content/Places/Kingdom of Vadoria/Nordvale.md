A town situated roughly central in the [[Kingdom of Vadoria]], and is adjacent to the [[Evervein]] river. It marks at the southern point of the [[Vale of Songs]] and the northernmost point of the [[Vale of Prayers]].

This old market town is home to numerous artisans and those peddling finer wares.

It is the location of shops owned by [[@Jack]] and [[@Splatts]].

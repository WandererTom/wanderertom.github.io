A huge inland saltwater lake that separates several nations in [[Alethyria]]. To the west of the lake is the [[Kingdom of Vadoria]], to the north the [[Jagged Peaks]] and the [[Dunren]], to the east the [[Nethirian Empire]], and to the south is [[Asantia's Blessing]].

The lake is shrouded by a thick, perpetual fog. Few who venture into the fog ever return.
